# Executive Summary

- Masan reported record 2Q2026 profitability. Net revenue rose 53.5% YoY to VND28,118 billion, EBITDA rose 87.1% to VND7,014 billion, NPAT Pre-MI increased 134.7% to VND3,800 billion, and NPAT Post-MI increased 202.8% to VND3,125 billion.
- For 1H2026, net revenue increased 40.1% YoY to VND52,137 billion, EBITDA increased 54.7% to VND11,990 billion, NPAT Pre-MI increased 121.9% to VND5,773 billion, and NPAT Post-MI increased 206.6% to VND4,371 billion.
- Masan proposed raising FY2026 guidance, subject to shareholder approval, to VND98,000-105,000 billion of revenue and VND10,000-11,500 billion of NPAT Pre-MI. The proposed high-case NPAT Pre-MI target is approximately 46% above the prior VND7,900 billion high case. Management also indicated approximately VND23,000 billion of FY2026 EBITDA at the new high case.
- The Consumer Operating System (cOS) generated VND20,059 billion of 2Q revenue, up 20.5% YoY, and VND3,356 billion of NPAT Pre-MI, up 26.2%. WCM delivered 27.4% revenue growth and VND142 billion of NPAT Pre-MI; MCH produced volume-led 14.2% revenue growth; and TCB profit contribution rose 19.2%.
- MSR was the largest earnings swing factor. Its 2Q revenue increased 5.0x YoY to VND8,138 billion and NPAT Pre-MI increased from VND6 billion to VND1,666 billion, supported by an average APT price of USD3,245/mtu and improved operations.
- Group Net Debt/LTM EBITDA improved to 2.4x from 2.7x at FY2025. Masan's stated capital-allocation order is deleveraging toward 2.0x, high-return cOS reinvestment, and then shareholder distributions. The Board intends to evaluate a cash dividend and/or buyback after the leverage target is reached.

# Segment Commentary

## WinCommerce

- 2Q revenue increased 27.4% YoY to VND11,629 billion and NPAT Pre-MI increased from VND10 billion to VND142 billion. The net margin expanded 110 bps YoY to 1.2%, supported by scale and cost discipline, although it narrowed QoQ because of seasonal energy costs.
- WCM added 324 net new stores in 2Q and reached 5,141 stores. More than 90% of the quarter's openings achieved store-level EBITDA breakeven during the quarter. The company remained on track for 1,000-1,500 net openings in FY2026.
- Minimart LFL sales rose 12.4%, the eighth consecutive quarter of double-digit growth. Supermarket LFL sales rose 16.3% following renovations and improved store productivity.
- Rural minimart LFL sales rose 26%; rural average daily sales reached approximately 90% of urban levels, and rural locations represented approximately 80% of the quarter's openings.
- WCM remained net cash positive and operated with one day of net working capital, allowing expansion to be funded without incremental balance-sheet pressure.

## Masan Consumer

- MCH delivered 2Q revenue of VND7,165 billion, up 14.2% YoY, with volume growth of 12.5%. Management described demand as resilient following the distribution transformation completed in 2025.
- Premiumization was led by CHIN-SU, whose revenue rose 27.0%, and Omachi, up 17.8%. Nam Ngu's fish-sauce volume recovered at a high-single-digit rate, while bottled beverages returned to 17.8% growth.
- Home and personal care revenue rose 32.5% from a small base, driven by Chante and Homey, while Global Business grew 24.0%.
- Gross margin was broadly stable at 44.6%, but net margin declined 80 bps YoY to 20.7% as logistics costs rose 120 bps to 4.4% of revenue.
- Retail Supreme expanded direct general-trade execution: sales rose 10.6%, active outlets reached approximately 550,000, SKUs per outlet reached approximately 5.8, and about 43,000 outlets sold more than six categories. Management is shifting attention from outlet addition toward productivity per outlet.
- The AGM-approved maximum dividend of VND5,000 per share implies a potential yield of approximately 3-4% at the company's assumed VND130,000 share price, although the actual payment depends on FY2026 performance.

## Masan MEATLife

- MML delivered 2Q revenue of VND2,683 billion and NPAT Pre-MI of VND191 billion, equivalent to a 7.1% margin. On a normalized basis excluding a 2025 non-cash gain from a renegotiated long-term supplier agreement, the margin improved 480 bps YoY.
- Fresh-meat revenue rose 15.2% and processed-meat revenue rose 34.7%; processed meat represented 37.6% of revenue, supporting the shift toward a higher-margin branded model.
- MML sales through WCM rose 30.5%, while LFL WCM-store average daily sales reached VND2.6 million, up 18.9%.
- Heo Cao Boi grew 21.2% and Ponnie grew 45.5%. Innovation products contributed 37.5% of branded-meat revenue.

## Phuc Long Heritage

- PLH's 2Q revenue increased 26.9% YoY to VND550 billion and NPAT Pre-MI increased 55.5% to VND67 billion.
- Standard-format LFL average daily sales reached VND26.2 million, up 18.5%, supported by delivery growth and better in-store productivity.
- PLH opened 15 standard-format stores in 2Q and reached 220 standalone stores, excluding WCM kiosks. It remained on track for 40-50 openings in FY2026, weighted toward 2H.

## Masan High-Tech Materials

- MSR delivered 2Q revenue of VND8,138 billion, up 404.1%, EBITDA of VND2,492 billion, up 342.7%, and NPAT Pre-MI of VND1,666 billion versus VND6 billion in 2Q2025.
- Average APT prices were USD3,245/mtu in 2Q, more than double the AGM planning range. Management linked demand to AI infrastructure, semiconductor-capacity expansion, and defense spending, while emphasizing MSR's position in non-China supply.
- EBITDA rose 4.4x as externally sourced tungsten feed supported refinery utilization during the West Pit transition. That feed carried lower margins; management expects margins to improve as internal production ramps.
- Standalone Net Debt/EBITDA was 2.1x in 2Q and is expected to fall to approximately 1.7x by end-2026 and move toward net cash by end-2027. Management indicated this could support dividends from 2027.
- Export-tax reductions for acid-grade fluorspar and bismuth cement are expected to add about USD2.5 million to 2H2026 earnings, or USD5.0 million annualized.
- Potential additions of 115 million tonnes across Nui Phao Expansion and Nui Chiem could extend mining and processing life by 20-30 years, subject to approvals. The GBI partnership provides third-party feedstock for a proposed expansion of tungsten-oxide capacity above 8,000 tonnes WO3.
- The planned HOSE migration is intended to broaden liquidity and market access and deepen strategic-investor engagement.

## Techcombank

- TCB contributed VND1,450 billion of profit share in 2Q, up 19.2% YoY, and VND2,766 billion in 1H, up 15.6%. Management described TCB as a stable contributor to group earnings quality.

## 1H Outlook

- 1H2026 actual results were complete at the release date. NPAT Pre-MI of VND5,773 billion represented 73% of the prior VND7,900 billion FY2026 high-case target, giving management confidence to propose higher full-year guidance.
- Management expects momentum to continue in 2H2026. The revised outlook remains sensitive to commodity prices, consumer sentiment, market conditions, and execution.
- WCM revenue growth in 1H was above the FY2026 guidance range, with management expecting momentum into the seasonally stronger second half. New-store openings remain targeted at 1,000-1,500 for the year.
- PLH openings are expected to be weighted toward 2H. MSR expects margin improvement as internal ore production ramps and lower-margin third-party feed becomes a smaller part of the mix.

# Investment View

## Positives

- Earnings growth is broadening across consumer-retail operations while MSR adds a large incremental profit pool. The combination produced materially faster EBITDA and NPAT growth than revenue growth.
- WCM's traffic-led LFL growth, rapid store-level breakeven, net-cash position, and one-day net working capital support the case that store expansion can be self-funded.
- MCH's growth was volume-led despite a post-transformation base, while premiumization and new categories broadened the growth mix.
- Leverage fell despite continued expansion, and management set an explicit 2.0x leverage threshold before considering cash returns to shareholders.
- MSR's improved earnings, deleveraging path, potential reserve additions, and planned processing expansion could improve the durability of cash generation beyond the current APT cycle.

## Areas to Monitor

- The proposed guidance increase depends heavily on MSR commodity-price realization and continued cOS execution. APT prices in 2Q were far above the AGM planning range, making the earnings base sensitive to normalization.
- MCH logistics costs rose materially and compressed net margin despite stable gross margin.
- WCM margin narrowed sequentially because of energy costs, even though traffic and revenue remained resilient.
- MML's reported NPAT Pre-MI declined YoY because of a prior-year non-cash gain; normalized operating improvement should be checked against cash conversion and recurring profitability.
- Group debt increased from FY2025 and the cash conversion cycle lengthened, so delivery of the stated deleveraging path remains important.
- The revised budget requires shareholder approval, while MSR expansion, mine-life additions, and the HOSE migration are subject to execution and/or regulatory processes.

# Catalysts

- Shareholder approval and subsequent delivery of the proposed VND10,000-11,500 billion FY2026 NPAT Pre-MI target.
- Continued WCM LFL growth, new-store rollout, and store-level breakeven supporting higher group-quality earnings.
- MCH premiumization, Retail Supreme productivity gains, and recovery in general-trade execution.
- MSR margin expansion as internal production ramps, continued elevated APT prices, export-tax benefits, and faster deleveraging.
- Approval of MSR's resource additions and expansion of tungsten-oxide capacity above 8,000 tonnes WO3.
- Potential MSR dividends from 2027 and MSN cash dividends and/or share buybacks after leverage approaches 2.0x.
- MSR's planned migration to HOSE, potentially improving liquidity and strategic-investor access.

# Risks

- APT and other commodity prices could fall materially from 2Q levels, reducing MSR earnings and slowing deleveraging.
- Weak consumer sentiment, market conditions, or execution could prevent delivery of the proposed FY2026 guidance.
- WCM expansion could pressure margins or capital efficiency if new stores take longer to reach breakeven.
- Higher logistics, energy, and other operating costs could offset volume growth at MCH and WCM.
- The West Pit transition and reliance on lower-margin third-party feed could delay expected MSR margin expansion.
- Resource additions, capacity expansion, export-tax benefits, and the HOSE migration are exposed to regulatory and implementation risk.
- Group debt rose in 1H and working-capital days increased; weaker cash conversion could delay the 2.0x leverage target and shareholder distributions.
