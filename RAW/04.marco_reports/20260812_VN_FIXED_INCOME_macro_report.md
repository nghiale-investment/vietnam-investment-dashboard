                     Bond Market Forecast

                     G-bond yields could remain high in August

                     We expect G-bond yields to remain elevated in August. The VST may increase bond offerings
                     after completing only 15.5% of its Q3 issuance plan and 40.2% of its full-year target by end-July,
                     while elevated global yields could continue to put upward pressure on the domestic curve.
                     However, improving banking-system liquidity should help limit the extent of any increase in G-
                     bond yields.
                     Money Market

                     Interbank rates continued to ease in July

                     Interbank liquidity improved in July, with the average ON rate declining to nearly 4.0% from 5.0%
                     in June. The ON rate fell to 0.5% on July 30 before rebounding to 6.5% on the final day of the
                     month due to month-end liquidity demand, ending July 75 bps below end-June.

                     Overnight rates eased during the month, mainly due to continued lower bank liquidity demand,
                     reflected in OMO outstanding continuing to decline by VND72.9tn (USD2.8bn) to VND172.5tn
                     (USD6.6bn). In July, the SBV injected VND105.9tn (USD4.0bn) through OMO operations, while
                     around VND178.8tn (USD6.8bn) of OMO repos matured, resulting in a net withdrawal of
                     VND72.8tn (USD2.8bn).
                     Outlook: We expect interbank liquidity conditions to remain ample, which should help ease

                     interbank rates. The SBV is likely to maintain flexible liquidity management through OMO, while
                     recent changes to the treatment of State Treasury term deposits in LDR calculations should help
                     ease banks' funding constraints. In addition, potential FX purchases by the SBV could provide
                     further VND liquidity to the banking system.

                     Foreign Exchange Rate
                     USD/VND remained broadly stable despite the widening trade deficit
                     The USD/VND rate remained stable throughout July, trading within a narrow range of 26,254–
                     26,335. The VND appreciated 0.1% MoM to 26,296 in the interbank market and has remained
                     broadly unchanged YTD. FX stability was supported by a 1.3% decline in the DXY, easing pressure
                     in the OTC FX market, and continued solid FDI inflows.
                     Outlook: The record-high trade deficit could put upward pressure on the USD/VND rate if
                     sustained. However, solid FDI inflows and easing pressure in the OTC FX market should help
                     mitigate depreciation pressure. As of August 11, the unofficial USD/VND rate had fallen to 25,710,
                     implying VND appreciation of 2.2% MTD and 4.3% YTD, while the interbank rate declined to 26,134
                     (+0.6% MTD and YTD for the VND).
