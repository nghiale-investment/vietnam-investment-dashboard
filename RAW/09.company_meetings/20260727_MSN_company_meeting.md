# Executive Summary

Masan Group presented 2Q2026 as a step-change quarter in which the Consumer Operating System (cOS) continued broad-based growth and Masan High-Tech Materials (MSR) added record strategic-materials earnings. Consolidated 2Q revenue reached VND28,118 billion, up 53.5% YoY; EBITDA reached VND7,014 billion, up 87.1%; NPAT Pre-MI reached VND3,800 billion, 2.3x the prior year; and NPAT Post-MI reached VND3,125 billion, up 3.0x. The cOS businesses generated VND20,059 billion of revenue, up 20.5%, and VND3,356 billion of NPAT Pre-MI, up 26.2%. MSR contributed VND1,666 billion of NPAT Pre-MI, up from VND6 billion.

Management proposed raising the FY2026 high-case NPAT Pre-MI target by approximately 46% to VND11,500 billion, implying NPAT Post-MI near VND8,000 billion and full-year EBITDA around VND23,000 billion. In the meeting, Danny Le said current tungsten economics could put actual NPAT Pre-MI around VND12,000 billion, but this was a management expectation rather than formal guidance. The revised outlook is heavily supported by high APT prices, making commodity sensitivity a central debate despite structural demand from AI infrastructure, semiconductors, and defense.

The balance sheet strengthened. Cash and equivalents were approximately VND17,557 billion, LTM free cash flow VND9,103 billion, and Group Net Debt/LTM EBITDA improved to 2.4x from 2.7x at FY2025. Management targets 2.0x over the medium term, potentially by end-2026 or within the following year. Around 80% of debt is long term and 20% short term. Recent VND borrowing costs were locked around 7.9-8.0% for the first year; USD borrowing was around 5.59%, with hedged cost below 9%. Management prioritized free cash flow for deleveraging, high-return reinvestment, and later shareholder distributions.

WinCommerce remained the retail engine. 2Q revenue rose 27.4% to VND11,629 billion and NPAT Pre-MI reached VND142 billion, up 14x. WCM added 324 net new stores in 2Q and 550 in 1H, reaching 5,141 stores. More than 90% of new stores reached store-EBITDA breakeven within the quarter or first year, and approximately 80% of openings were rural. Management maintained the full-year target of 1,000-1,500 new stores, with a high case around 1,500. LFL growth remained double digit; supermarkets were particularly profitable, while southern minimarts still recorded a small store-level loss and require assortment and operating changes in 2H.

WCM described unusually consistent operating leverage: revenue has risen from around VND8.5 trillion in 3Q2024 to more than VND11 trillion in 2Q2026, with management expecting more than VND13 trillion in 3Q2026. The company said it had improved quarterly profit by roughly VND150 billion YoY for ten consecutive quarters. July LFL growth remained double digit despite rapid store expansion. In a northern province with heavy competitor openings, WCM still reported LFL above 10%, supporting management's view that its customer proposition differs from competitors.

Retail Supreme continued to deepen GT distribution for MCH. Active selling outlets rose from around 430,000 in 1Q to 550,000 in 2Q; multi-category outlets increased from 33,000 to 43,000; and SKUs per order rose from 5.5 to 5.8, with a target of seven. Management said MCH directly reaches about 450,000 of roughly 500,000 off-premise outlets and is expanding selectively into a much larger on-premise universe. Wholesaler contribution has fallen from around 60% to 30% but should remain around 20-25% long term.

MCH revenue grew 14.2%, led by CHIN-SU, Omachi, beverages, HPC, and international business. CHIN-SU grew 27%, Omachi 17.8%, beverages 17.8%, HPC 32.5%, and international business 24%. Management emphasized premiumization at approximately USD5,000 GDP per capita and intends to move from export-only distribution toward building regional and local brands in Southeast Asia. It expects MCH revenue and profit growth to become more synchronized in 2027 as Retail Supreme and brand investments mature.

PLH revenue grew 26.9% to VND550 billion; EBITDA grew around 40% and NPAT 55.5% in the presentation. PLH opened 15 stores, reaching about 220 standalone stores, and will focus on smaller formats, brand building in Hanoi, and eventual international expansion.

MML generated VND2,683 billion of 2Q revenue and VND191 billion of NPAT Pre-MI. Fresh meat grew 15.2%, processed meat 34.7%, and branded-meat growth was around 25%. New products contributed roughly 40% of branded-meat growth, led by MEATDeli, marinated products, meat snacks, Heo Cao Bồi, and Ponnie. Average meat sales per comparable WCM store reached around VND2.6 million per day, up about 19-20%. A key runway is raising branded-meat bill penetration in rural WCM stores from around 3-4% toward the 6-7% urban level through a more suitable rural assortment.

The digital engine is intended to turn cOS into a data-driven operating system across place, product, price, and promotion. Masan is building applications around SAP/Oracle ERP, a unified data platform, location scoring, product scoring, end-to-end supply chain, and employee workflows. Management said 70-80% of current effort is data cleaning and organization. A location-intelligence platform is being integrated into the full store-opening process so lease terms and field feedback enrich the scoring model. By September 2026, WCM expects about 90% of order value to be centrally and automatically ordered through WiNARE, up from around 20% two years earlier. Management estimated 30% less store workload and around VND300 billion of annual cost capacity, while official commentary estimated approximately VND100 billion of annual savings for each 10 percentage points of automation.

MSR was the main incremental profit engine. 2Q revenue reached VND8,138 billion, five times the prior year; EBITDA increased 4.4x; and NPAT Pre-MI reached VND1,666 billion. Average APT price was USD3,245/mtu, well above the AGM case. MSR's Net Debt/EBITDA fell to about 2.1x and is officially targeted around 1.7x at year-end, with management suggesting performance could be better. The company expects a path toward net cash in 2027 and potential dividends thereafter.

MSR's structural plan includes potential addition of approximately 115 million tonnes of resources at Núi Pháo Expansion and Núi Chiêm, which could extend mining and processing life by 20-30 years subject to approval; scaling tungsten oxide capacity beyond 8,000 tonnes WO3; and importing feed from about 20 third-party mines. The GB Innovation partnership adds Korean tungsten feed. Third-party concentrate expands throughput but carries lower margins than internally mined ore, so revenue and absolute profit can rise faster than margin.

Management plans to migrate MSR from UPCoM to HOSE, potentially with an update at end-2026 or early 2027. It views the transfer and engagement with a strategic investor, together with MSR free cash flow, as possible funding sources for the USD700 million The CrownX put obligation due around end-2027; management said it does not intend to rely primarily on additional borrowing.

Other Q&A covered MSR resource tax, government inspection, inventory, and Nyobolt. MSR said it has fully provisioned its estimated resource-tax obligation and hopes to resolve the methodology in 3Q2026. A 2023 government inspection was described as complete with no material issue beyond administrative and ongoing tax matters. MSR inventory exceeded VND8 trillion, including about VND1.1 trillion of APT/tungsten inventory carried at roughly USD2,000-2,100/mtu versus a market price near USD3,000 at the meeting. Masan partially reversed an impairment on Nyobolt after a USD60 million fundraising at a USD1 billion valuation and improved cash-flow outlook, while retaining a provision for prudence.
