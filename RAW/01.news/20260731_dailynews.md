# Daily News - 2026-07-31

## News Summary

Vietnam equities ended July with profit-taking after the prior rebound, but state-owned banks continued to support the index. SSI's midday note said the VN-Index initially rose for a fourth consecutive session, up 0.57% to 1,754.22, led by VCB, BID and CTG on better-than-expected Q2 results, while profit-taking began to cool the rally. SSI's market wrap later said the VN-Index reversed 0.51% to 1,735.78, with SOCBs still anchoring the market: VCB rose 5%, BID 2% and CTG 1.2%. Vietcap's wrap similarly recorded a 0.51% VN-Index decline, combined turnover of USD773mn, down 5% day on day, and foreign net selling of USD13.5mn. Foreigners bought VCB, FPT and VIC but sold VHM, TCB and VPB. VIC was the main index drag, while VCB and FPT still drew foreign inflows.

Macro and banking liquidity items were material. SHS said the interbank overnight rate fell sharply to 1.3%, down 0.9 percentage points from the prior session and far below the late-June level near 13%, reflecting a rapid two-week liquidity easing. SSI's August 3 morning note, referring to 27-31 July, said the SBV injected a net VND12.3tn while the overnight rate fell to 0.75%, its lowest since March 2024, although longer-tenor rates remained high because of the persistent credit-deposit gap. SHS and SSI both flagged SBV Decision 1743/QD-NHNN, which raises the portion of State Treasury term deposits counted in total deposits for LDR calculations from 20% to 50%, effective 1 August 2026 through 31 July 2028, mainly benefiting state-owned banks. Vietcap also noted US PCE fell 0.1% MoM in June and core PCE rose 0.1% MoM, while US Q2 GDP grew 1.5% annualized, below consensus.

Company news was dense across banks, Vingroup, retail, aviation, ports, fertilizer and real estate. SHS said VIC first-half consolidated profit after tax reached VND20.375tn, 4.5 times YoY and 58% of plan, while VHM reported record first-half profit after tax of VND52.092tn, up nearly 380% YoY and almost 87% of the full-year profit plan. Vietcap said VCB rose 5-6% after Q2 PBT increased 58% YoY; first-half TOI and PBT reached 56% and 55% of its forecasts, and Q2 included a VND1.9tn provision reversal related to corporate bonds booked in Q4 2025. CTG first-half TOI rose 25% YoY and PBT 37%, reaching 51% of Vietcap's forecast. SSI said HDB Q2 PBT reached VND7.1tn, up 50.5% YoY and 16.2% QoQ, supported by strong fee income, subsidiary capital-contribution income and lower provisioning, while loans rose 20% YTD to VND706tn. SSI said STB Q2 PBT fell 44.5% YoY to VND2.0tn as provisioning rose to VND5.1tn, while asset quality deteriorated and group 2 loans rose sharply. HSC said OCB Q2 PBT rose 25% YoY to VND1.2tn, with first-half PBT of VND2.5tn, up 31% and equal to 44% of forecast, but put its rating and target price under review.

Outside banks, SSI said KBC Q2 revenue reached VND589bn, up 16% YoY, but NPAT-MI fell 96% to VND16bn and first-half NPAT-MI reached only VND240bn, or 10% of forecast. Vietcap said GMD Q2 NPAT-MI reached VND1.133tn, up 155% YoY, helped by a VND609bn one-off divestment gain likely related to the CJ restructuring deal; estimated core NPAT-MI was VND636bn, up 17% YoY. DPM reported first-half revenue of VND12.6tn, up 34% YoY, and NPAT-MI of VND1.3tn, 2.2 times YoY, but Vietcap saw downside risk to the 2026 forecast. HDG first-half revenue was VND1.17tn, down 1% YoY, core NPAT-MI was VND334bn, up 18%, and reported NPAT-MI was VND100bn, down 27%, implying slight downside risk. ### 14:13 - Manulife - SSI
#SSI Research - STB 2Q26 Business results

STB reported 2Q26 PBT of VND 2.0tn (-44.5% YoY, -3.6% QoQ), falling short of our expectations. While PPOP surged to VND 9.95 tn (+28.1% YoY, +32.0% QoQ), supported by strong growth in net fee income of VND 3.2 tn (+248% YoY, +327% QoQ), bottom-line earnings were significantly weighed down by a sharp increase in provisioning expenses, which rose to VND 5.1tn (x5.6 times YoY).

Key takeaways:

• Credit growth remained relatively muted compared with peers. Total loans reached VND 636tn as of 2Q26 (+1.5% YTD). In contrast, funding growth accelerated, driven primarily by customer deposits, which increased 7.8% QoQ to VND 647.9tn.

• Asset quality deteriorated materially. Group 2 loans surged to VND 16.8tn, or 2.8x QoQ, while NPLs climbed to VND 48 tn (+15.6% QoQ). Consequently, the NPL ratio increased to 7.54% as of 2Q26, from 6.62% in 1Q26. Notably, since 3Q25, bad debt has increased by approximately VND 31.3 tn, whereas cumulative provisioning expenses amounted to only VND 17.4tn over the same period. As such, we believe provisioning pressure is likely to remain elevated in the coming quarters as the bank strengthens its balance sheet and improves its LLR, which stood at 56.7% in 2Q26.

• NIM showed a recovery. NIM improved to 3.09% in 2Q26 (+18bps QoQ), although still below 3.54% in 2Q25. The sequential improvement was mainly driven by a more favorable repricing dynamic, with asset yields rising 85bps QoQ, outpacing the increase in funding costs of 65bps QoQ.
