# Daily News - 2026-07-30

## News Summary

Vietnam equities rallied sharply on 30 July, led by banks, Vingroup names, FRT and broader risk-on breadth. Vietcap's midday note said the VN-Index surged 1.8% to 1,735.48 in the morning, with turnover up 69% day on day and foreign investors net buying USD16mn, led by banks VCB, BID, CTG, VPB and TCB along with VHM. Vietcap's market wrap later recorded a 2.35% gain to 1,744.66, combined turnover of USD813mn, up 42% day on day, and foreign net buying of USD25.2mn. Top foreign buys were VIC, VNM and MSN, while TCB, ACB and NVL were the largest sells. The wrap said breadth was overwhelmingly positive with 277 advancers, 58 decliners and 19 ceiling closes, with VHM the largest index contributor followed by VCB, BID, CTG and TCB. HSC also flagged estimated ETF rebalancing for 31 July, with biggest expected buys in HPG, PNJ, GMD, MCH and NLG and biggest expected sells in TPB, MSB, MBB, TCB and VPB.

Macro and rates news centered on the Fed and domestic liquidity expectations. Vietcap said the Fed held rates at 3.5-3.75%, in line with expectations, but called the decision a hawkish hold because three of twelve FOMC members dissented in favor of a 25 bp hike. Markets were pricing more than a 57% chance of a September hike, which could pressure VND, though Vietcap noted lower domestic gold prices and solid FDI inflows should limit FX weakness. Bank shares were also supported by market talk that the SBV might increase the share of State Treasury term deposits counted in LDR calculations.

Company news was led by VRE, FRT, DGW, DCM, DMX, MCH, VHM and MWG. HSC said VRE delivered Q2 net profit of VND1.609tn, up 30.4% YoY, and revenue of VND2.374tn, up 10.8%, beating expectations on non-operating items; first-half results fulfilled 70.1% of HSC's FY26 net-profit forecast and HSC maintained Buy with a VND40,600 target. Vietcap said DCM first-half revenue rose 27% YoY and NPAT-MI rose 55%, completing 61% and 59% of forecasts, while Q2 revenue rose 11% and NPAT-MI rose 36%, though urea ASP, gas cost and science-fund expense risks remained. DGW first-half revenue rose 40% YoY and NPAT-MI rose 130%, reaching 49% and 79% of Vietcap's forecasts, implying potential upside. FRT rose 7% after first-half revenue rose 33% YoY and NPAT-MI rose 119%, with Long Chau revenue up 33% and NPAT-MI up 84%, and FPT Shop revenue up 32% with profitability maintained. HSC and Vietcap both noted DMX had been approved to list on HOSE, with first trading on 6 August at a VND80,000 reference price and a +/-20% first-day trading band. Vietcap also said MCH would trade ex-rights on 10 August for a VND2,000/share cash dividend, the first tranche of an expected VND6,000/share FY2026 dividend. VHM first-half NPAT-MI rose 4.7 times YoY and reached 88% of Vietcap's forecast, while MWG first-half NPAT reached VND6.0tn, up 88.4% YoY, with Q2 NPAT doubling to VND3.3tn and beating expectations.
