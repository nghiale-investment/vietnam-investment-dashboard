### 13:17 - Manulife - SSI
SSIRS - Decision 40 (effective on 5 August) on managing, restructuring, and optimizing State capital

• Prime Minister's Decision 40 (effective on 5 August) marks a notable shift from the 2021-25 privatization and divestment agenda toward a broader framework for managing, restructuring, and optimizing State capital. The new classification system reaffirms State control in strategic sectors, including energy, strategic minerals, digital infrastructure, data platforms, high technology, and innovation. Agribank remains designated for full State ownership, while VietinBank is required to maintain a minimum State stake of 65% (currently 64.46%).

• Although the decision primarily applies to enterprises in which the State directly holds capital, its implications extend to subsidiaries through mandatory five-year restructuring plans. State-owned parent companies are required to review ownership structures across subsidiaries and affiliates, while retaining discretion to maintain holdings in businesses that support core operations, strengthen value chains, or serve strategic functions within the group.

• The implementation timeline is clear. Ownership representatives must submit or approve 2026-30 restructuring plans within 30 working days of the decision taking effect, with nationwide consolidation scheduled for completion in 4Q26. In our view, the key significance of Decision 40 lies not in accelerating divestment, but in establishing a clearer framework to address long-standing ownership, and capital allocation challenges across the SOE sector, which might include the free-float issue for listed SOEs and their subsidiaries. This could provide a more structured pathway for resolving legacy ownership issues while improving the efficiency of State capital management over time.
