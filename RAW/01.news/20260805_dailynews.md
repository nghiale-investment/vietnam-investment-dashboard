### 09:59 - Manulife - SSI
MoF and SBV are taking step-by-step measures to ease banking system liquidity.

Last Friday, July 30, the SBV issued Decision 1743 officially increasing the cap on State Treasury deposits counted toward banks' funding base in LDR calculation from 20% to 50%.

Earlier, Resolution 168, issued in June 2026, also allowed the Ministry of Finance to proactively determine the cap for placing idle State Treasury funds at commercial banks, which can exceed 50% of the total idle State Treasury balance. The MoF is now reviewing and amending Decree 24 to provide greater flexibility in managing limits on term deposits of State Treasury funds, instead of applying a fixed cap.

These measures are expected to help ease current liquidity constraints within the banking system, benefiting particularly state-owned commercial banks at which State Treasury funds are deposited and potentially real estate developers whose credit growth is being curbed.

SSI said these measures reflect the government's direction to keep monetary policy accommodative.

Banks have rebounded, with state-owned commercial bank stocks outperforming peers over five days: VCB +9.7%, CTG +8.9% and BID +7.5%, versus VNFIN +4.6%.

Mid-cap property developers also bounced back 5-10%.
