## Strong Qc for H2 2026, strong volume and CGM price in 2027

- We raise our target price (TP) for NT2 by 1% to VND29,000/share and reiterate our BUY rating. Our higher TP is mainly due to (1) our 7% higher 2026-30F aggregate reported NPAT-MI projection (respective changes of +23%/+3%/+7%/+6%/-2%) and (2) 5% QoQ higher net cash balance, which outweigh (3) our 1 ppt increase in risk-free rate from 6.0% to 7.0%.

- Our higher 2026-30F aggregate reported NPAT projection is mainly due to (1) 2% higher aggregate sales volume, (2) 1% lower gas price on average and (3) 33-34% lower maintenance expenses for 2026-27F (in accordance with Circular 99 which removes the advanced booking accounting policy).

- We forecast 2026F reported NPAT-MI to decline slightly (-6% YoY), due to (1) lower FX loss compensation receipt, (2) a 34% YoY lower price spread, (3) 37% YoY higher SG&A expenses, and (4) a doubling tax rate to 20%, which outweigh (5) 39% YoY sales volume growth, (6) ~VND390bn YoY lower depreciation expenses and (7) 30% YoY lower maintenance expenses.

- We forecast 2027F core NPAT-MI to grow 6% YoY to VND1.1tn from 2026 high base, driven by (1) 24% YoY higher financial income on a stronger gross cash balance (VND4.4tn end Q2 2026, 74% of mkt cap), (2) 1% YoY sales volume growth, and (3) a 1% YoY higher price spread, supported by sector CGM price expected to grow 18% YoY.

- NT2’s valuation is attractive with a 2026F PER of 6.1x, 57% lower than the 5Y average of comparable single power plant peers (14.3x). Its TTM P/E is at a historic low.

- Downside risks: Lower-than-expected price spread.

- Upside: Capacity expansion with Nhon Trach 5 or higher-than-expected dividend yield Higher 2026F Qc and sales volume on stronger H2 mobilization to lift 2026 earnings: We raise our 2026F Qc volume forecast by 15% to 4.1bn kWh (+16% YoY) and our sales volume forecast by 8% to 4.3bn kWh (+39% YoY), following H2 Qc allocation of 2.2bn kWh, per industry players. MOIT estimates power consumption to grow 6-11% YoY in H2 2026 vs H1 (10% YoY). Additional low-cost gas from the Hai Thach-Moc Tinh expansion gas field helps to improve NT2’s competitive edge. NT2 expects the additional gas supply from Hai Thach-Moc Tinh to enable NT2 to use 100% domestic gas at least in the next five years (completely no LNG). We cut our forecast for NT2’s new average gas price in 2026-2030 by 1% to USD10.9/MMBTU, which is 23% and 9% lower than our projected LNG price and gas price in Southeast region (page 4). We cut our 2026F–2027F maintenance cost forecasts by 33%–34%, as NT2 no longer makes advance provisions for major maintenance expenses following the adoption of Circular 99. This accounting change contributed to a 54% YoY decline in maintenance expenses in H1 2026. We expect maintenance costs to rebound from 2028F, when NT2 undertakes its next major maintenance cycle, with the related costs subsequently allocated over the following five years.

## Earnings Outlook

We raise our 2026-30F aggregate reported NPAT forecast by 7% (respective changes of +23%/+3%/+7%/+6%/-2%). Our higher 2026-30F aggregate reported NPAT projection is mainly due to (1) 2% higher aggregate sales volume, (2) 1% lower gas price on average and (3) 33-34% lower maintenance expenses for 2026-27F (in accordance with Circular 99 which remove the advanced booking accounting policy).

Incremental Hai Thach–Moc Tinh gas helps improve NT2’s competitive edge. NT2 released detailed materials seeking shareholders’ written approval for Appendix No. 13 to

its gas purchase agreement with GAS. The appendix governs NT2’s purchase of incremental gas supply from the Hai Thach–Moc Tinh expansion project. The expansion field is expected to supply a total output of 11.0bn m³ during 2026–2040 for the southeast region, in which NT2 will potentially get a major portion.

The input gas price is USD7.38/MMBTU (excluding transportation tariff), escalating by 2% annually and without the FO price linkage. We estimate a 2026 delivered gas price of USD8.97/MMBTU (including transportation and distribution cost). We estimate NT2’s new average gas price in 2026-2030 at USD10.9/MMBTU, which is 1% lower than our previous forecast (despite having raised our average Brent oil price and average FO price forecasts by 2% and 7%, respectively, over the same period, in our Energy Sector Update, dated July 24, 2026).
