MWG - 2026 NPAT target likely to be achieved 2–3 months
early; DMX sales, BHX sales per store to remain stable in
H2 vs H1 - Analyst Meeting Note
Company Research 04 Aug 2026
We attended MWG analyst meeting on Tuesday. Our key takeaways are as follows.
MWG to complete 2026 NPAT target 2-3 months ahead of schedule
Financial Performance: On track to achieve the 2026 NPAT target of VND9,200bn,
potentially 2-3 months earlier than anticipated. H1 NPAT was VND6,100bn, nearly
doubling YoY. Online sales across the group were nearly 10% of total revenue in H1 2026.
Capital Allocation: First cash dividend tranche of 10% to be paid on August 6 while the
second tranche is planned in October 2026. Higher cash dividends are expected in 2027.
A share buyback cannot be executed in 2026 due to procedural constraints but is under
consideration for 2027.
An Khang Pharmacy: Achieved profitability for four consecutive months starting in March
2026 and is expected to be profitable for full-year 2026. The chain will accelerate
expansion starting late 2026.
DMX: Strong momentum maintained amid strong industry growth, market share gains, and
services ramp up
H2 2026 outlook: Confident in achieving 30% full-year revenue growth. Key seasonal
drivers: cooling season, back-to-school, iPhone 18 launch, and year-end shopping. Consu
mer front-loading not observed. H2 revenue is expected to be maintained at absolute
levels, similar to H1.
More details on H1 2026 financial performance: Q2 2026 revenue +1% QoQ, NPAT up 20%
QoQ, despite Q1 being typically a high season. H1 NPAT achieved VND4,876bn (+73%
YoY). 2026 NPAT now expected to surpass VND10,000bn vs the original target of VND7,
350bn.
Margin expansion: Net margin surpassed 8% for first time in Q2 2026 (+3 ppts YoY to
8.1%) – purely due to GPM. This was driven by market-wide price increases (small
impact), higher-margin services, cost optimization, and trade-up effects from
installment programs - mostly sustainable from structural changes.
Category performance (H1 2026): Apple phones +50%, Android phones +15%, laptops
+35%, accessories +30%, TVs +20%, air conditioners +60%, washing machines +25%,
refrigerators +35%, and home appliances +35% YoY.

Apple partnership: Achieved USD1.2bn in Apple revenue over four years (September
2023-August 2026), completing the company’s USD1bn target one year early. New fiveyear target set at USD2.2bn by 2030.
Consumer finance: 38% of revenue transacted through installment plans (vs 35% in
2025). DMX holds ~80% market share in consumer finance for electronics and
appliances.
DMX technician services: H1 revenue was nearly VND1,900bn (+47% YoY). Revenue from
external customers (not purchasing from DMX) exceeded 10% of total technician
revenue. 670,000 air conditioners were installed in H1 with installation productivity up
20%.
Online and SuperApp: H1 online revenue was 11% of total revenue, to accelerate in H2
with campaigns. Official SuperApp launch is planned Q4 2026 and is under heavy
preparation.
EraBlue (Indonesia): H1 2026 revenue was up 92% YoY with 17% SSSG. NPAT +154% YoY,
accumulated losses fully cleared, which is a key milestone. The chain operates 261 stores
as of June 30, targeting 500 stores by early 2027 and 1,000 stores before 2030 – the
latter being a conservative target. 80% of locations for the company’s 500-store plan
secured. Despite 2-3 year upfront rent practices, the average store payback is 15
months.
IPO and listing: Successfully raised ~USD500mn at a 93% subscription rate, comprised of
90% institutional investors. The listing is on August 6, 2026. MWG ownership reduced to
~86% post-IPO.
Dividend policy: Total expected dividend yield of 10% (VND8,000/share) within the 12
months. First dividend of VND4,000/share from 2025 earnings paid post-listing (record
date August 18-19, payment August 26). Second dividend of VND4,000/share from 2026
earnings in two tranches: VND2,000 December 2026 and VND2,000 after the April 2027
AGM. A 2027 stock split at a minimum 1:1 ratio is planned.
Long-term incentive plan: Stock option program for fewer than 15 senior executives.
Executives must purchase shares at VND80,000. Program delivers value only if
challenging five-year targets met: revenue and profit KPIs (not yet disclosed) plus
doubling of market cap. Maximum dilution of 10% over five years. Full scheme under
consideration, to be approved at the Q4 2026 EGM – MWG will abstain from voting on this
matter.
BHX: SSSG returned to a postive single-digit level in Q2, NPM improved to 3.4%, can exceed
1,000 new stores for 2026
H1 2026 performance and outlook: H1 revenue +25% YoY, Q2 accelerated to 31% YoY. Q1
NPAT was ~VND400bn. Q2 NPAT was VND510bn. H1 total NPAT was VND910bn. On track
for full-year 2026 target of VND1,800bn. Remaining accumulated losses total VND7tn.

Management expects to fully eliminate these losses within the next 2–3 years before
proceeding with BHX's IPO and listing.
Store expansion: Opened 632 new stores in H1 2026 to hit ~3,100 stores. Early stores
achieved profitability 3-6 months post-opening. Maintains target of 1,000 new stores for
full-year 2026, with potential to exceed the plan. 50% of new stores in the south, 50% in
north and central regions combined. 2026 cohort already achieved positive store-level
EBIT and began covering DC costs by June. 2025 cohort (~800 stores) continues
generating positive profit after DC costs. Pre-2025 cohort (~1,700 stores) delivered
single-digit revenue growth with Q2 2026 store-level EBIT up over 30% YoY.
Northern expansion: Northern store openings are progressing smoothly. Initial supply
chain and consumer behavior challenges are minimal and manageable. Average revenue
per northern store is ~VND1bn/month, meeting expectations.
Category performance: Fresh category growth achieved 7%-17% and FMCG 15%-40%
YoY in Q2 2026. Online revenue was VND2,200bn (+85% YoY). Fresh products account for
~30% of revenue.
Margins and cost management: Shrinkage rate reduced to below 3%. Logistics cost ratio
continues improving despite higher fuel costs – driving the strong margin expansion in H1
2026.
