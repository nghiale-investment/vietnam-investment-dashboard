## Market wrap: VN-Index sees marginal decline on Monday

The VN-Index saw a marginal decline of 0.1% (-1.6 pts) on Monday to close the trading day at 1,727.5. The index has declined for three consecutive trading days. Throughout the index, there were 151 gainers, 154 laggards, and 66 unchanged stocks. Total trading volume substantially decreased from the previous trading day to USD530.4mn.

- In Banking, LPB (-2.9%), VPB (-1.4%), and ACB (-1.4%) sharply fell, while STB (+2.6%) and HDB (+1.5%) surged.

- Consumer stocks MWG (+1.5%), FRT (+2.9%), VNM (+1.0%), and SAB (+1.0%) climbed. In contrast, DMX (-1.4%) and HVN (-1.5%) fell.

- Oil & Gas stocks BSR (+2.6%), PLX (+1.3%), and PVD (+1.1%) posted strong gains.

- Notable laggards in other sectors included Real Estate firms VIC (-1.2%) and BCM (-2.4%), Chemicals company DGC (-4.6%), Plastics company BMP (-1.1%), and Utility firm REE (-0.8%). Standout gainers inlcuded Real Estate firms VRE (+1.9%) and KDH (+1.7%), Utility company GAS (+4.1%), Rubber producer GVR (+0.5%), and Tech company FPT (+0.7%).

## NT2: 16% lower H1 2026 audited NPAT on temporary reversion of accounting method

- NT2 revised down its H1 2026 audited NPAT by 16% (~VND95bn) to VND488bn (+34% YoY, vs +61% YoY in pre-audit results), due to VND119bn of additional periodic maintenance expenses recognized in the period.

- This adjustment is related to a 54% YoY decline in H1 2026 maintenance expense (pre-audited), following the adoption of Circular 99, which removes the advanced booking accounting policy for major maintenance costs.

- According to NT2, the VND119bn adjustment reflects the auditor's recommendation for NT2 to book this expense under the previous (pre-Circular 99) advanced booking method for H1 2026, pending clarification from the Ministry of Finance (MoF) on the application of Circular 99. Once the MoF responses, NT2 will adjust this maintenance expense back in line with the pre-audited financial statements treatment.

- We see insignificant changes to our 2026F NPAT forecast (VND1,064bn, -6% YoY), as this is a temporary, procedural accounting treatment pending MoF clarification rather than a reversal of the underlying decline in maintenance costs under Circular 99. We currently have a BUY rating for NT2 with a target price of VND29,000/share.
