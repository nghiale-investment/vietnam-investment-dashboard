## Market wrap

## VN-Index declines 0.5%

The VN-Index declined 0.5% (-8.9 pts) on Friday to close the trading week at 1,735.8. Performance was mixed among large-

caps, with 18 stocks falling in the VN30 group and 10 rising. Throughout the index, there were 133 gainers, 204 laggards, and 49 unchanged stocks. Total trading volume decreased from the previous trading day to USD722.6mn.

- State-owned banks VCB (+5.0%), BID (+2.0%), and CTG (+1.1%) surged following positive Q2 earnings results, while private banks TCB (-1.2%), ACB (-2.0%), LPB (-1.5%), and HDB (-1.9%) sharply fell.

- In Real Estate, VIC (-2.7%) and BCM (-1.7%) plunged, as VRE (+2.1%) posted a strong gain and VHM (+0.1%) marginally advanced.

- In the Consumer sector, FRT (+7.0%) hit the upper limit and MSN (-2.7%), MCH (-1.3%), PNJ (-4.6%), and HVN (-1.4%) tumbled.

- Noteworthy laggards in other sectors included Financial Services firms SSI (-1.9%) and HCM (-2.7%), Steelmaker HPG (-0.5%), Utility company POW (-0.7%), and Oil & Gas companies BSR (-1.4%), PLX (-1.6%), and PVD (-4.0%).

Banking Sector - SBV relaxes deduction rate of State treasury deposit balances in

LDR calculation - On July 30, the State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) issued Decision No.1743, allowing credit institutions to lower the deduction rate applied to State Treasury term deposit balances from 80% to 50% in LDR calculation (i.e., raising the portion of State Treasury deposits counted in the LDR calculation from 20% to 50%).

- The Decision is effective from August 1, 2026, to July 31, 2028 (two years). Our view:

- Positive for the sector. This should help to lower compliance pressure for banks and support the interest rate environment. SOE banks including VCB, CTG, and BID stand out to directly benefit the most. - Per our sources and estimates, the State treasury term deposit balance in late July is around VND700tn (USD26.6bn). The adjustment (approximately VND200tn/USD7.6bn) would help to ease the LDRs of the three State-owned banks, each by around 1.8-2.4 ppts.
