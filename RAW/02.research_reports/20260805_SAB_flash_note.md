SAB - A&P spending, Q1 front-loading aftermath weigh on
Q2 earnings, management optimistic on H2 outlook -
Analyst Meeting Note
Company Research 05 Aug 2026
* Q2 & H1 2026 results & outlook:
- Revenue: Revenue rose 1% YoY in Q2 2026, as lower beer volume was attributed to the
aftermath of the front-loading effect in Q1 ahead of portfolio-wide ASP hikes in April and May.
Overall, H1 2026 revenue grew 6% YoY, with management describing H1 as a positive period.
- Beer selling price increases: SAB initiated two hikes in Q2- a planned increase on April 1 and
an additional smaller increase on May 1 to recover cost spikes following the Middle East
conflict. Management believes pricing actions for 2026 are sufficient barring raw-material
shocks, while pricing is back at parity with competitors, who had raised their portfolio-wide
ASP beforehand in late 2025.
- A&P: The sharp Q2 A&P increase was attributed to the non-recurring World Cup, alongside
the renewal of the national football team sponsorship and promotional campaigns for the
ASEAN Championship. Management expects A&P costs to cool down to normal levels in H2
2026.
- GPM: Q2 gross margin expansion was supported by the two selling price increases (April and
May), alongside favorable malt and rice costs from SAB's full-year 2026 hedging, while
aluminum costs remained well hedged in H1 2026 despite current price volatility. Regarding
aluminum, SAB has hedged through H2 2026 and is currently working further with can
suppliers to firm hedging positions for Q1–Q2 2027.
- Market share: SAB closed the share gap with its closest competitor in Q2 after the Tet
season. Management attributed the widening gap to (1) SAB's mainstream-skewed portfolio
being less suited to gifting demand, which is a major Tet driver, and (2) competitors having a
head start in 250ml cans, a format the market was already shifting toward. Meanwhile, Bia
Saigon remains the clear No. 1 beer brand in Vietnam on sell-out volume, per third-party
research.
- Regarding the H2 2026 outlook, management has not provided formal guidance but
expressed a positive view, citing strong July momentum and healthy summer volumes.
Management expects beer demand to remain solid into H2, supported by the ongoing ASEAN

Championship, while A&P spending should normalize after the Q2 spike. However,
management did note some caution on cost-of-living pressures among consumers and
retailers.
* Other notes:
- Regarding portfolio innovation, the 250ml baby can is now SAB's new growth driver: Saigon
Chill 250ml, launched in July 2025, delivered triple-digit YoY volume growth; while 333 Pilsner
250ml, launched in April 2026, posted close to triple-digit volume growth in its first months;
and Bia Saigon Export Premium 250ml — a mainstream SKU — was recently launched in June
following the success of the two aforementioned SKUs. Management framed the market's
shift from 330ml to 250ml as affordability-driven rather than premiumization, and is evaluating
a 250ml mainstream launch. Although the current contribution of the new 250ml SKUs to total
volume remains relatively modest, they deliver higher margins per liter than larger cans, which
we expect to support SAB's profitability going forward.
- Regarding the distribution channel, management has seen a General Trade (GT) recovery
since the beginning of 2026 after the 2025 disruptions from the mandatory e-invoicing led
many household businesses to close. Management indicated GT remains its crucial channel
given SAB’s nationwide and rural reach, and GT carries higher margins, benefiting from
returnable-bottle economics. Meanwhile, Modern Trade (MT) and e-commerce are growing
faster in terms of revenue though profitability is lower than GT. In particular, B2C e-commerce
GMV grew 109% YoY as of June 2026, with sub-premium up 214% YoY and mainstream up 83%
YoY, while SAB is also the No. 1 beer brand on Shopee and Lazada. Additionally, SAB became
the No. 1 beer company in the MT channel for the first time in April 2026.
- Regarding exports, H1 2026 export volume grew triple digits, with the footprint expanding to
over 40 countries. Core foreign markets are ASEAN countries along with Singapore’s shipchandling market; newer traction is in Japan, Taiwan, Korea, and Mongolia. Although exports
contribute only a single-digit share of SAB's total volume, revenue and profit, SAB targets
double-digit contribution within five years.
- Regarding management, Mr. Johnathan Ooi Siew Loke has been appointed as the new CFO.
He reaffirmed SAB's existing strategy and commitment to maintaining a substantial dividend
policy.
