DPG wins VND1.3tn Quy Nhon–Pleiku Expressway contract

- DPG announced that it won Package XL.03 under the Quy Nhon–Pleiku Expressway with a contract value of VND1.32tn and construction period of 42 months. This project is classified as a nationally important project, with total investment of VND27.6tn funded by public investment capital.

- DPG noted that VND635bn of the contract value will be performed by subcontractors (potentially assigned to DPG’s associates, DP1 and DP2).

- Including this package, DPG’s 2026 YTD newly signed contract value stands at approximately VND5.1tn, in line with our projection and equivalent to 77% of our full-year forecast of VND6.6tn. We view the contract award as broadly in line with our expectations and as supporting the construction segment’s revenue visibility.

- We currently have a BUY rating for DPG with a target price of VND48,900/share. DPG is trading at 2026 and 2027 P/Es of 9.7x and 7.0x, respectively. FRT: Long Chau pilots diagnostics business with immaterial earnings contribution in 2026

- Long Chau launched a pilot diagnostics business, offering three categories of laboratory testing services: (1) routine health tests (including complete blood count, blood glucose, lipid profile, and liver and kidney function tests), (2) cancer screening, and (3) comprehensive health check-up packages. Long Chau offers both basic and premium packages for these services, with the premium packages covering a broader range of diagnostic parameters.

- Under its asset-light business model, FRT is responsible for customer acquisition, medical consultation, sample collection, and customer service, while partner laboratories conduct the laboratory analysis. The company is partnering with Gene Solutions (a provider of molecular diagnostics and genetic testing services) and LabHouse (a private provider of routine clinical laboratory testing). Initially, FRT is leveraging its existing vaccination centers as sample collection points, utilizing its network of doctors and nurses, while also offering home sample collection within a 15 km radius. FRT currently operates five sample collection centers across Ho Chi Minh City and Dong Nai. The company stated it will continue expanding its network to serve customers in more localities nationwide, though specific expansion details have not yet been disclosed.

- Overall, this development is in line with the company’s strategy to develop Long Chau as a healthcare ecosystem. Given that the business remains in the pilot phase, we expect its earnings contribution to be immaterial in 2026. We will factor in the contribution of diagnostics business as the rollout progresses and more concrete operating metrics become available.

- We currently have a BUY rating for FRT with a target price of VND180,100/share.

KDH: Positive first launch result at Gladia high-rise phase, with presales at nearly VND4tn (USD152mn), in line with our expectations

- The Gladia’s high-rise phase (commercial name: Gladia Heights) is the condo component of ‘Gladia by the Waters,’ an 11.8-ha joint-venture project between Keppel and KDH in HCMC. The high-rissse phase comprises three towers, with a total of 616 condos and 23 shophouses.

- Saturday, August 1, marked the official first-launch event of the Gladia high-rise phase, releasing ~500 condos across two towers. During the launch event, more than 90% of the launched units were presold, generating nearly VND4tn (USD152mn) in presales.

- The company expects to complete presales of the remaining units in the Gladia high-rise phase over the next three months. According to management, guided revenue for all high-rise units is approximately VND6tn (USD228mn) and profit is around VND2.0tn (USD76mn; before allocation to minority interests) with revenue recognition expected to begin in 2027G, upon handovers.

- The strong presales result of the Gladia high-rise phase reaffirms KDH’s strong reputation and resilient demand in core HCMC’s supply-constrained primary market amid overall cautious homebuyer sentiment.

- The first launch presales for the Gladia high-rise is equivalent to nearly 60% of our full-year 2026F presales forecast for KDH. We foresee insignificant changes to our 2026F presales forecast for KDH (VND6.7tn/USD254mn; +12% YoY).

## 1. Preliminary 7M 2026 results: Preliminary 7M 2026 revenue was VND19.3tn (+41% YoY), with

July revenue of VND3.2tn (+28% YoY). Growth was driven by office equipment (+150% YoY) and home appliances (+72% YoY), while laptop and tablet revenue remained flat MoM at ~VND1.05tn.

## 2. Outlook:

- Q3 2026 guidance: DGW guides for VND7.66tn of net revenue (+4% YoY; +5% QoQ) and VND165bn NPAT (-1% YoY; -47% QoQ), reflecting a conservative base case. Management expects no further GPM expansion in H2 2026 vs Q2 2026, as the benefit from selling lower-cost laptop inventory accumulated earlier was largely realized in Q2, while laptop selling price increases are expected to moderate in Q3 vs Q2.

- Segmental outlook: - Laptops & mobile phones: Based on third-party research, product price increases are expected to moderate in Q3 vs Q2 but continue at 6%-7% per quarter through end-2027. Management expects 2027 laptop and mobile phone sales to remain broadly flat YoY or post modest YoY growth (i.e., with higher ASPs largely offsetting potential softer volume growth), rather than decline as seen in the post-COVID correction in 2023 (laptop revenue -16% YoY; mobile phone revenue -26% YoY). DGW believes any cyclical slowdown should be milder than during COVID given significantly lower inventories stocked by retailers. - Servers: Around 2/3 of server revenue comes from large enterprise customers (e.g., Viettel, FPT Telecom, CMG, Sovico, and Phenikaa). DGW expects enterprise server investment to remain solid in 2027, with current limited visibility beyond 2028. - FMCG: Management expects demand in H2 2026 to remain flat to slightly weaker vs H1 2026.

- New product additions in Q3 2026: - Hisense: + On August 3, DGW and Hisense signed a comprehensive strategic partnership covering: (1) E-commerce: Operation of official Hisense (TVs, refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners) and Toshiba TV brand shops on Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop. (2) Air conditioners: Nationwide distribution of Hisense air conditioners. (3) B2B solutions: Distribution of Hisense's commercial display solutions for the education and healthcare sectors, as well as solar energy storage solutions. (4) After-sales services: Warranty and after-sales support for the full Hisense product portfolio. + Revenue contribution begins in August 2026. - Intel: In August, DGW plans to become an official Intel CPU distributor in Vietnam, completing its PC component portfolio for desktop PC assembly.
