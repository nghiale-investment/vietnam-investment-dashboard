DGW - New brand additions to support growth amid
potential laptop sales cooldown - Analyst Meeting Note
Company Research 04 Aug 2026
1. Preliminary 7M 2026 results: Preliminary 7M 2026 revenue was VND19.3tn (+41% YoY), with
July revenue of VND3.2tn (+28% YoY). Growth was driven by office equipment (+150% YoY)
and home appliances (+72% YoY), while laptop and tablet revenue remained flat MoM at
~VND1.05tn.
2. Outlook:
* Q3 2026 guidance: DGW guides for VND7.66tn of net revenue (+4% YoY; +5% QoQ) and
VND165bn NPAT (-1% YoY; -47% QoQ), reflecting a conservative base case. Management
expects no further GPM expansion in H2 2026 vs Q2 2026, as the benefit from selling lowercost laptop inventory accumulated earlier was largely realized in Q2, while laptop selling price
increases are expected to moderate in Q3 vs Q2.
* Segmental outlook:
- Laptops & mobile phones: Based on third-party research, product price increases are
expected to moderate in Q3 vs Q2 but continue at 6%-7% per quarter through end-2027.
Management expects 2027 laptop and mobile phone sales to remain broadly flat YoY or post
modest YoY growth (i.e., with higher ASPs largely offsetting potential softer volume growth),
rather than decline as seen in the post-COVID correction in 2023 (laptop revenue -16% YoY;
mobile phone revenue -26% YoY). DGW believes any cyclical slowdown should be milder than
during COVID given significantly lower inventories stocked by retailers.
- Servers: Around 2/3 of server revenue comes from large enterprise customers (e.g., Viettel,
FPT Telecom, CMG, Sovico, and Phenikaa). DGW expects enterprise server investment to
remain solid in 2027, with current limited visibility beyond 2028.
- FMCG: Management expects demand in H2 2026 to remain flat to slightly weaker vs H1 2026.
* New product additions in Q3 2026:
- Hisense:
+ On August 3, DGW and Hisense signed a comprehensive strategic partnership covering:
(1) E-commerce: Operation of official Hisense (TVs, refrigerators, washing machines, air
conditioners) and Toshiba TV brand shops on Shopee, Lazada, and TikTok Shop.

(2) Air conditioners: Nationwide distribution of Hisense air conditioners.
(3) B2B solutions: Distribution of Hisense's commercial display solutions for the education and
healthcare sectors, as well as solar energy storage solutions.
(4) After-sales services: Warranty and after-sales support for the full Hisense product
portfolio.
+ Revenue contribution begins in August 2026.
- Intel: In August, DGW plans to become an official Intel CPU distributor in Vietnam, completing
its PC component portfolio for desktop PC assembly.
- Automotive consumables: DGW began distributing Shell lubricants (the third-largest
lubricant brand in Vietnam) in July 2026, covering both B2C (garages and retailers) and B2B
(industrial customers) channels. Management estimates Vietnam's lubricant market value at
USD1bn, growing at an 8% CAGR. DGW targets VND400bn revenue in H2 2026 (in line with our
forecast of VND395bn), accelerating in 2027 and reaching VND2.6tn revenue (~USD100mn;
~10% market share) by 2028. Management highlighted the 30% market share held by
unbranded products as an opportunity. Over time, this business could expand into adjacent
categories such as tires, batteries, and automotive parts.
3. FY2026 cash dividend: On August 4, DGW announced an ex-rights date of August 17 for
obtaining shareholders’ opinions on paying a FY2026 cash dividend of VND3,000/share (7.6%
yield). Per DGW, this dividend is expected to be paid in two tranches. The first tranche is
scheduled for October 2026, while the second tranche is expected in May–June 2027.
4. Our view:
* We view the addition of new brands as supporting DGW's medium-term earnings growth,
helping partly offset a potential normalization in laptop sales in H2 2026-2027 following the
high base in H1 2026. Among the new additions, we believe Shell offers the strongest longterm earnings potential, given its sizable addressable market (USD1bn for lubricants alone,
with opportunities to expand into adjacent automotive categories) and a relatively less
competitive market structure than the ICT segment.
* Overall, we see potential upside to our 2026F forecasts, pending a more comprehensive
review.
* We currently have an OUTPERFORM rating for DGW with a target price of VND45,400/share.
