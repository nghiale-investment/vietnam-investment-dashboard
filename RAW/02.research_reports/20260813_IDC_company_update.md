 Recovery underpinned by land bank, dividend appeal
 • We reiterate our BUY rating on IDC, while trimming our target price (TP) by 3% to
  VND51,200/share.
 • Our lower TP is mainly driven by a higher WACC assumption of 14.9% (vs 14.3% previously)
  following a 100-bps increase in our risk-free rate assumption to 7.0%, while broadly
  maintaining our 2026–27F NPAT-MI forecasts.
 • We forecast NPAT-MI to reach VND2.0tn (USD75mn) in 2026F and VND2.3tn (USD86mn) in
  2027F, implying YoY growth of 3% and 14%, respectively. We expect earnings growth to be
  driven by: (1) a recovery in land sales and handovers from existing IPs, such as Huu Thanh, Phu My
  2, and Phu My 2 Expansion, alongside expected new launches at Vinh Quang and Tan Phuoc 1;
  (2) continued IP ASP growth; and (3) initial contributions from Huu Thanh UA from 2027F.
 • IDC’s valuation is undemanding with 2026/27F adjusted P/Bs of 1.4/1.2x, ~21/30% lower than
  its 3Y median adjusted P/B of 1.8x. In addition, we believe IDC offers an attractive and

  sustainable dividend yield, with a projected VND3,000/share payout implying an 8.8% yield,
  supported by its 22% net cash-to-equity ratio at end-Q2 2026 and average annual IP cash
  inflows of VND4.6tn (USD174mn) over 2026–28F.
 • We believe IDC remains well positioned to attract tenants, backed by its sizeable and cleared
  land bank in strategic locations and a diversified tenant base including Hyosung, PepsiCo, and
  Heineken.
 • Downside risks: Slower-than-expected IP land sales; adverse developments in US tariffs on
  Vietnam.
 IP land sales recovery supported by active projects and upcoming launches: For 2026/27F,
 we forecast IP land sales of 100 ha/120 ha (+31%/+21% YoY), driven by recovering sales at current

 IPs (including Huu Thanh, Phu My 2, and Phu My 2 Expansion), and expected new launches at
 Vinh Quang and Tan Phuoc 1 from Q4 2026F. The two new IPs began infrastructure construction
 in Q1 2026 and are expected to contribute ~19% of our 2026–27F IP land sales forecast.
 Meanwhile, we project IP land handovers of 80 ha/95 ha in 2026/27F (+14%/+19% YoY), driven by
 our estimated end-Q2 2026 unbilled backlog of ~63 ha in addition to new land sales.
 Residential earnings to pick up from 2027F, driven by the commercial launch of the Huu
 Thanh UA project (47 ha; Tay Ninh): We project the residential segment’s gross profit to remain

                     Valuation

                     We value IDC using a sum-of-the-parts (SoTP) approach to capture the company’s IP and
                     residential land value, as well as contributions from the energy and toll-collection segments. For
                     the IP and residential segments, we apply the RNAV method using DCF, which we view as the
                     most appropriate and commonly used valuation approach. For the energy segment, we adopt a
                     P/E comparable method, given our expectation of relatively stable medium-term profitability.

                     We lower our target price by 3% to VND51,200/share, mainly reflecting a higher WACC
