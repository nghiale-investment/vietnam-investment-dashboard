 VHC: July revenue down 10% YoY on high base from last year’s US front-loading

 * July 2026 revenue declined 10% YoY, mainly due to a 36% drop in the US market from a high
 comparison base in July 2025 resulting from front-loading activities ahead of the US reciprocal
 tariff last year. Meanwhile, Europe remained strong, with revenue growing 19% YoY as China
 recovered 80% YoY from a low base. We attribute this to the tightening global supply of sea
 whitefish, along with shrinking Chinese tilapia output — pangasius’s closest substitute in the
 same segment — amid low selling prices, disease outbreaks, and stricter farming controls under
 China’s revised Fisheries Law.
 * By segment, frozen filets and related products declined 8% YoY, driven by a decrease of 21% of
 frozen filet sales, mainly dragged by US sales, while value-added products improved notably

 (+108% YoY) from a low base. In contrast, the collagen & gelatin (C&G) segment posted a sharp
 42% YoY decline, suggesting a weakening sales trend in this segment.
 * In 7M 2026, revenue was VND7.5tn (+6% YoY), completing 57% of our full-year forecast and in
 line with our expectations, driven by growth across markets, including the US (+8% YoY), Europe
 (+7% YoY), China (+13% YoY), and alternative markets (+18% YoY).
