CTR: 7M 2026 PBT continues momentum with 18% YoY growth, in line with

expectations 7M 2026 results: Revenue was VND9.41tn (+28% YoY) in 7M 2026, while PBT was VND475.9bn (+18% YoY), fulfilling 61% and 55% of our respective full-year forecasts. Revenue remained ahead of our expectations, mainly driven by better-than-expected Construction (+50% YoY; 63% of our full-year forecast) and Integrated solutions & technical services (+58% YoY; 70% of our full-year forecast). Additionally, Operation (+7% YoY; 56% of our full-year forecast) and

Infrastructure leasing (+14% YoY; 54% of our full-year forecast) are tracking below our expectations. Monthly highlights:

- Infrastructure leasing: As of end-July, CTR’s tower portfolio remained at 12,000 BTS, broadly unchanged from end-2025, indicating continued subdued new tower additions relative to the company’s 2026 target. However, the number of shared towers increased to 596 from 573 at end-June.

- Construction: Regarding its construction backlog, CTR secured VND304bn of new residential construction contracts, including VND156bn from B2B & SME customers and VND148bn from B2C customers.

- Integrated solutions & technical services: CTR signed 207 MWp of new solar capacity (+55% YoY), bringing solar coverage to 1,473 out of 3,321 communes nationwide, equivalent to 44% coverage.

- Social housing and housing for armed forces: CTR has completed regulatory procedures for the Viet Yen Riverpark project in Bac Ninh (6.04 ha) and the project in Thai Nguyen (0.76 ha); while evaluating demand and investment opportunities for future developments in Hanoi, Gia Lai, Bac Ninh, and Lang Son.

- Newly appointed CEO: Mr. Tran Trung Hung officially took over as CEO on July 31, 2026, ensuring continuity in CTR's operations and growth strategy.
