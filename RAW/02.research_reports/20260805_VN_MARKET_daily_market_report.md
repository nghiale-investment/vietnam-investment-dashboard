PHR: Foreign ownership limit reduces to 0% from 49%; guided to be lifted up again

- On August 4, 2026, PHR announced that its maximum foreign ownership limit (FOL) had been reduced to 0% from 49% previously, according to its official disclosure to the State Securities Commission (SSC) and the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange (HOSE). This is effective from August 4, 2026.

- Based on our discussion with the company, the adjustment was mainly attributable to two registered business activities subject to foreign ownership restrictions: 1) retail petroleum sales that PHR no longer operates but remain listed in its charter and business registration; and 2) electricity transmission and distribution related to its operating solar power business.

- Management indicated that the company is amending its charter and enterprise registration with removal of the FOL restricted business activities, with completion guided in Q3 2026G. They also expect the foreign ownership cap to be reinstated, subject to regulatory review and approval.

- Other notes: - Regarding portfolio innovation, the 250ml baby can is now SAB's new growth driver: Saigon Chill 250ml, launched in July 2025, delivered triple-digit YoY volume growth; while 333 Pilsner 250ml, launched in April 2026, posted close to triple-digit volume growth in its first months; and Bia Saigon Export Premium 250ml — a mainstream SKU — was recently launched in June following the success of the two aforementioned SKUs. Management framed the market's shift from 330ml to 250ml as affordability-driven rather than premiumization, and is evaluating a 250ml mainstream launch. Although the current contribution of the new 250ml SKUs to total volume remains relatively modest, they deliver higher margins per liter than larger cans, which we expect to support SAB's profitability going forward. - Regarding the distribution channel, management has seen a General Trade (GT) recovery since the beginning of 2026 after the 2025 disruptions from the mandatory e-invoicing led many household businesses to close. Management indicated GT remains its crucial channel given SAB’s nationwide and rural reach, and GT carries higher margins, benefiting from returnable-bottle economics. Meanwhile, Modern Trade (MT) and e-commerce are growing faster in terms of revenue though profitability is lower than GT. In particular, B2C e-commerce GMV grew 109% YoY as of June 2026, with sub-premium up 214% YoY and mainstream up 83% YoY, while SAB is also the No. 1 beer brand on Shopee and Lazada. Core foreign markets are ASEAN countries along with Singapore’s ship-chandling market; newer traction is in Japan, Taiwan, Korea, and Mongolia.
