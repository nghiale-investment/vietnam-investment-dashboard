DatVietVAC: Direct-to-consumer (D2C) expansion to support guided 16% NPAT
 before MI CAGR over 2025-30G - Investor Meeting Note

 We attended DatVietVAC (DVV)’s roadshows in Ho Chi Minh City and Hanoi on August 11–12,
 2026. Management discussed H1 2026 results, 2026–30F targets, strategy, and responses to
 investor questions.

 1. H1 2026 results & 2026 guidance:
 * H1 2026 results:

 - Net revenue was VND1,366bn (-7% YoY; 40% of 2026G) and NPAT before MI was VND135bn
 (+5% YoY; 32% of 2026G).
 - DVV operates two main segments: its content hub and communication services (which is a
 lower margin revenue stream). Net revenue declined 7% YoY mainly due to 12% YoY lower
 communication services revenue, while content hub revenue was broadly flat (+1% YoY).

 However, slight GPM improvement in communication services and SG&A expense optimization
 supported 5% YoY NPAT before MI growth.

 * 2026 guidance:
 - Under the base case, DVV targets net revenue of VND3,382bn (+6% YoY) and NPAT before MI
 of VND420bn (+11% YoY).

 - Given the H1 2026 results, management remains committed to its 2026G base-case targets,
 supported by: (1) seasonality, with entertainment activities and brand marketing spending
 typically concentrated in H2; (2) a higher number of shows and concerts in H2 vs H1; and (3) strong
 sponsorship progress (DVV has secured 85% of its internal sponsorship KPI for three H2 shows and
 60% for six H2 concerts, with concert sponsorship typically secured closer to event dates).

 - Management sees potential upside of 15%–35% on top of 2026G NPAT before MI guidance,
 driven by: (1) stronger-than-expected growth of key intellectual properties (IPs) including Tinh
 Ha Say Hi; (2) Gia Lai and Government projects; and (3) event management and out-of-home
 (OOH) advertising in partnership with Saigontourist.
 2. 2026–30 guidance:

 * 5Y base-case guidance: DVV targets gross revenue and NPAT before MI to grow at 9% and 16%
 CAGRs, respectively, over 2025–30G. Growth is mainly driven by a 15% CAGR in content hub revenue,
 with D2C’s revenue contribution rising from 13% of gross revenue in 2025 to 20%-30% in 2030G.

 * 5Y strategy: Management identified five key strategies, with D2C as the key growth driver over
 2026–30:
 - (1) Diversify show genres and audiences: Maintain dominant position among the 13–30 age
 group while developing blockbuster programs targeting audiences below 13 and aged 35–60.

 - (2) Diversify concert/event monetization: Continue mega concerts around DVV shows and
 expand into artist concerts, fan meetings, music festivals, and international events in Vietnam.
 - (3) Diversify artist monetization: Develop young artists as a talent pipeline for Say Hi and other
 IPs; accelerate D2C through concert tours/fan meetings, artist social media monetization, and
 exclusive fan interactions via subscription/donation.

 - (4) Accelerate IP monetization: Expand digital monetization and DVV’s own IP-related products,
 develop high-quality IPs, and target international IP exports.
 - (5) New business initiatives: Partner with international players, particularly from the US and

 South Korea, to train artists/music bands targeting debut in 2028–29, and develop a fandom
 platform for direct fan interaction.
 * 5Y “unicorn” scenario guidance: DVV targets 30% and 33% CAGRs for gross revenue and
 NPAT before MI, respectively, over 2025–30G. Key growth drivers include: (1) fully monetizing the
 “fan economy”; (2) strategic partnerships with consumer goods and service companies; and (3)
 commercializing artists through talent ownership and training.

 * Cash dividend:
 - DVV commits to a cash dividend of at least VND2,500/share in Q4 2026 (~5% dividend yield
 based on the IPO offering price).

 - Going forward, DVV targets an annual dividend payout ratio of ~50% (dividends equivalent to
 ~50% of NPAT).

 3. Discussions:
 * IPO proceeds (VND611bn) to be spent on:

 - Talent management: Prioritize partnerships with strategic investors, including potential South
 Korean partners, to establish joint ventures (JVs). Funding would support DVV’s JV contribution,
 artist training programs, curriculum development, and studio/facility investment.

 - D2C platform: Reduce margin leakage from third-party ticketing fees and improve
 understanding of end-user behavior to customize future products/services (supporting D2C
 revenue growth).
 - M&A: Pursue M&A opportunities across the media & entertainment value chain.

 * Artist reputation risk:
 - DVV conducts detailed background screening before inviting artists to shows and may consult

 relevant Government authorities as an additional layer of risk management.
 - DVV also continuously monitors artists’ public image and professional conduct. Management
 believes current measures keep artist-related reputational risk at a manageable level, while its
 media network and communication partners provide capabilities to manage potential incidents.

 4. Vietcap Research view:
 * DVV trades at ~13x 2026F post-money P/E based on DVV’s guidance, below the regional peer
 median of ~16x 2026F P/E and ~17x the 3-year average of the peer median P/E.

 * DVV delivered a superior ROE of 41.2% in 2025 vs regional peers, expected to remain strong at
 34.5% in 2026F after factoring in IPO proceeds, based on our forecasts. Meanwhile, the company
 targets a solid 16% CAGR in NPAT before MI over 2025-30G under the base-case scenario,
 providing room for potential attractive and sustainable dividend payouts in the future.

 * DVV plans to pay cash dividend of at least VND2,500/share (~5% dividend yield at IPO price),
 with payment planned for Q4 2026.
