## Market wrap

## VN-Index falls 0.7%

The VN-Index fell 0.7% (-11.7 pts) on Thursday to close the trading day at 1,764.8. Banks continued performing

poorly, dragging on the index. Throughout the index, there were 110 gainers, 213 laggards, and 50 unchanged stocks. Total trading volume substantially decreased from the previous trading day to USD575.8mn.

- Banks CTG (-1.6%), TCB (-1.5%), VPB (-1.4%), MBB (-1.6%), LPB (-1.9%), STB (-1.4%), and ACB (-1.3%) plunged.

- In Real Estate, VIC (-0.5%), VRE (-4.3%), BCM (-1.4%), NVL (-1.4%), and KBC (-1.6%) sharply fell. In contrast, VHM (+0.8%) climbed.

- Consumer stocks MSN (-1.0%), SAB (-1.6%), MWG (-1.4%), FRT (-2.1%), HVN (-3.6%), and PNJ (-3.8%) tumbled.

- Noteworthy laggards in other sectors included Industrials firms GEE (-2.9%) and VTP (-3.1%), Utility companies GAS (-1.0%) and POW (-2.9%), Rubber producer GVR (-1.1%), and Oil & Gas company PLX (-1.6%). News & Flash Note

News  & Flash Notes

NLG: Key takeaways from analyst meeting

We attended NLG's analyst meeting on August 5, comprising a management presentation, Q&A session, and site visit to the Elyse Island project (Dong Nai). Key discussion points included H1 2026 presales results (as also stated in our July 30 Earnings Flash), the H2 2026 outlook, and CEO transition.

- H1 2026 presales: Presales rose 52% YoY to VND5.1tn (USD193mn), driven by Sol Garden (25% of total), Mizuki Park (20%), Izumi City (18%), and Southgate (14%).

- 2026 guidance: Management reaffirmed the AGM-approved 2026 targets of NPAT-MI of VND720bn (USD27mn; +3% YoY) and presales of VND23.46tn (USD890mn; +98% YoY).

- H2 2026G expected launches: Next phases of Sol Garden, Izumi City, Mizuki Park, Southgate, and Can Tho.

- Potential stake sales/commercial land sales: Management is in discussions with potential partners for Izumi City and Waterpoint Phase 2, with no further details given.

- CEO transition: Chairman Mr. Nguyen Xuan Quang stated that the CEO transition is a normal management succession and does not reflect any change in the company's strategic direction. The Chairman and the Board of Directors for the 2026–2031 term remain unchanged. Overall, with H1 2026 NPAT-MI and presales reaching only 17% and 35% of our full-year forecasts, respectively, we foresee downside risks to our 2026F NPAT-MI forecast of VND710bn (USD27mn; +1% YoY) and presales forecast of VND14.4tn (USD546mn; +21% YoY), pending a more comprehensive review. VNM: Continuing to benefit from broad-based market share gains, premiumization, economies of scale - Analyst Meeting Note

We attended VNM’s analyst meeting on August 6. Overall, the dairy giant has reaffirmed its return in gaining market share. Management remains positive on VNM’s outlook, while being general in disclosing the breakdown of data. Our key takeaways are as follows. H1 2026 Highlights:

- Revenue: VND34,996bn, up 17.8% YoY.

- Net profit after tax: VND5,643bn, up 38.4% YoY.

- Gross profit margin: 43.1%, up 200 bps YoY. Economies of scale and product mix are key factors: Vinamilk has spent significant expenses on developing and marketing new and premium products to consumers over the last three years. Now that sales volumes of these new products are growing fast, the company has seen a strong increase in margins. Outlook and Guidance:

- H2 2026 gross margin outlook to remain stable at levels similar to H1 2026, with no significant fluctuations anticipated. Raw material costs have been locked in through year-end. Input cost increases remain within manageable levels.

- No further price increases are planned: A modest 3-4% price increase was implemented in early April 2026. The strategy focuses on sharing challenges with consumers while driving revenue growth through volume expansion and product mix enhancement.

- Q3 export momentum to remain stable vs Q2. Continuous operations were maintained through alternative shipping routes via the Strait of Hormuz and Jordan. Domestic Market:

- Domestic revenue up 12.4% YoY (high-single-digit volume growth). Q2 domestic revenue increased 6.5% YoY – Q2 2025 was not a low base like Q1 2025.

- Vinamilk gained market share in many categories in H1 2026 compared to both YoY and end-2025 levels, including fresh milk, but did not disclose the data.

- Core categories (liquid milk, yogurt, adult milk, infant formula) maintained steady growth.

- The premium product revenue share now exceeds a double-digit percentage, higher than last year’s high-single-digit level. These lines expanded distribution, introduced new SKUs, and received positive market response.

- GreenFarm: Triple-digit growth rate.

- Optimum A2 Pro+: After only six months, significantly contributed to the Optimum portfolio with unprecedented growth rates. SKU expansion continues

- Moc Chau Creamery: High-end brand successfully penetrating southern markets through supermarkets and Vinamilk stores, core finished products grew nearly double-digit in H1. Domestic sales channels: Increasing independence, GT shrank to 65% of sales

- General Trade (GT): 65% of revenue. Despite the declining share, GT remains critically important for at least the next five years. Growth has resumed after restructuring efforts.

- Modern Trade (MT): 19% of revenue, showing significant growth.

- Direct-to-Consumer (D2C): 15-16% of revenue, comprising nearly 950 proprietary stores (targeting 1,000 by year-end) and e-commerce, delivering substantial growth.
