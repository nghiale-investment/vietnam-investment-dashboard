Company Research 01 Aug 2026
* ACV reported Q2 2026 net revenue of VND6.3tn (broadly flat YoY; -8% QoQ) and NPAT-MI of
VND3.4tn (+29% YoY; broadly flat QoQ). This brought H1 2026 net revenue to VND13.2tn (+4%
YoY; 48% of our 2026F forecast) and NPAT-MI to VND6.7tn (+17% YoY; 53% of our 2026F
forecast).
* H1 2026 results were broadly in line with our expectations. However, we are reviewing our
2026 earnings forecast, particularly the assumed commencement date of Long Thanh
International Airport (LTH). If the airport begins commercial operations as scheduled in late
2026, the initial operating and depreciation expenses could pose downside risk to our current
forecast, pending a fuller review.
* Passenger traffic remained resilient despite the exclusion of Phu Quoc Airport (PQA) from
ACV’s 2026 operating base following its transfer to Sun Group. Q2 2026 total passengers
reached 28.8 million (-5% YoY; -9% QoQ), including 11.3 million international passengers (+3%
YoY) and 17.5 million domestic passengers (-9% YoY). In H1 2026, total traffic still edged up 1%
YoY to 60.5 million, as international passengers increased 2% YoY and domestic traffic
remained broadly flat. This suggests that traffic growth across ACV’s remaining airport
network more than offset the loss of PQA traffic.
* Q2 2026 GPM expanded to 64.2% (+2.4 ppts YoY; +0.7 ppt QoQ), when COGS declined around
7% YoY amid broadly flat revenue. The improvement mainly reflected lower staff costs, which
more than offset higher depreciation. H1 2026 GPM was 63.9% (-1.2 ppts YoY), above our
2026F forecast of 58.5%.
* Financial income increased 10% YoY and 93% QoQ to VND289bn in Q2 2026, bringing H1 2026
financial income to VND440bn (-14% YoY; 40% of our 2026F forecast). Meanwhile, financial
expenses reversed to a VND42bn net gain in Q2 2026, vs an expense of VND739bn in Q2 2025,
mainly due to a roughly VND60bn reversal of provisions for financial investments, alongside
favorable FX movements. As a result, reported Q2 PBT increased 29% YoY, while PBT excluding
net FX gains remained broadly flat YoY at VND3.9tn.
* As a reminder:
- Following the transfer of Phu Quoc Airport (PQA) to Sun Group, PQA has been excluded from
ACV’s operating base in 2026. Our forecasts already incorporate the recurring earnings impact
of the transfer but do not include any potential one-off compensation gain. ACV has

completed the asset valuation and submitted a compensation proposal to the relevant
authorities; as final terms remain pending approval, we will incorporate any gain once there is
greater clarity on timing and amount. At end-2025, PQA’s net book value stood at VND762bn vs
a historical cost of VND3.2tn.
- Separately, following the transfer of the aviation-security segment to the Ministry of Public
Security in 2025, ACV has presented revenue and earnings from this segment as a separate
line item in its 2025 audited and 2026 financial statements.
