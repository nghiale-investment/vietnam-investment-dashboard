KBC - H1 2026 earnings plunge 80% YoY on weaker IP land
handovers, higher financial expenses - Earnings Flash
Company Research 01 Aug 2026
KBC reported Q2 2026 net revenue of VND589bn (USD22mn; +16% YoY, -56% QoQ) and
NPAT-MI of VND16bn (USD0.6mn; -96% YoY, -93% QoQ). We attribute the sharp earnings
decline mainly to limited IP land handovers during the quarter and higher-than-expected
financial expenses.
For H1 2026, KBC recorded net revenue of VND1.9tn (USD73mn; -46% YoY) and NPAT-MI
of VND240bn (USD9.1mn; -80% YoY), completing 23% and 10% of our respective full-year
forecasts. We see downside risk to our 2026F NPAT-MI forecast of VND2.4tn (USD92mn;
+38% YoY), pending a fuller review.
IP segment: KBC handed over ~20 ha in H1 2026, mainly at Nam Son Hap Linh IP, Que Vo II
Expansion IP, industrial clusters in Hung Yen, and Tan Phu Trung IP. According to KBC, its
unbilled IP land backlog stood at ~170 ha as of end-Q2 2026, of which at least 100 ha is
expected to be handed over in H2 2026. We estimate Q2 2026 IP land sales at ~10 ha,
down from ~30 ha in Q1 2026.
Residential (UA) segment: H1 2026 segment revenue was primarily driven by social
housing handovers.
