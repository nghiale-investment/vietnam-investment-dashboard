## Market wrap

## VN-Index starts week on positive note

VN-Index traded choppily in the first session of the week before closing 0.49% higher. Market breadth was notably

positive, with 245 advancers outpacing 88 decliners on the HOSE, including 13 stocks hitting their ceiling prices. Total trading value came in at around USD677mn.

- Banking and Financial Services stocks led the market higher, supported by TCB (+5.6%), VPB (+3.4%), TCX (+1.8%), SSI (+2.7%), and VIX (+2.6%).

- Oil & Gas and Utilities also performed strongly, with BSR (+2.7%), PLX (+4.3%), GAS (+7.0%), and POW (+1.8%) advancing.

- Materials and Industrials saw broad-based gains, led by GVR (+6.9%), DPM (+2.5%), PHR (+2.0%), GEX (+6.3%), GEE (+3.6%), VGC (+6.9%), and PVT (+5.7%).

- Consumer Goods and Consumer Services also strengthened, supported by SAB (+3.9%), MWG (+2.8%), FRT (+3.8%), and DGW (+2.3%).

- Real Estate traded mixed, with VIC (-3.0%), VHM (-1.9%), and CRV (-3.8%) under pressure, while BCM (+7.0%), TCH (+4.2%), DXG (+3.3%), and IJC (+6.9%) advanced.

## News

MWG: DMX July 2026 revenue remains strong, management's new NPAT

expectation exceeds our forecast DMX posted its 7M 2026 business results with total revenue of VND75,474bn, up 29% YoY driven by 30% SSSG, completing 58% of our full-year forecast of VND131tn, in line with our expectations.

July revenue hit VND10tn (+16% YoY); we expect revenue growth to normalize due to a higher base in H2 2025. Management is confident that Q3 2026 results will surpass the Q1 and Q2 2026 average, and that full-year 2026 NPAT will surpass VND10tn - exceeding our forecast of VND9.3tn.

## Pillar 1: Quality-driven growth, including retail categories

- ICT category: Apple phones revenue grew 50% YoY. Android phones revenue grew 15% YoY. Laptops grew 35% YoY. Accessories grew 30% YoY.

- CE category: Growth was supported by summer seasonality, with AC revenue accelerating to +55% YoY. Other product segments maintained steady momentum: home appliances grew +30% YoY, fridges grew +30% YoY, and washing machines grew +20% YoY.

## Pillar 2: Consumer finance & utility services

- Financed sales grew 47% YoY. Financed sales accounted for 38% of DMX total revenue in 7M 2026 vs 35% in full-year 2025.

- For utility payments and agent banking services, total gross merchandise value (GMV) was VND65tn over 43 million transactions in 7M 2026, growing 7% YoY and 11% YoY, respectively.

## Pillar 3: DMX Technician (Tho DMX)

- Total segment revenue reached VND2,177bn in 7M 2026, up 43% YoY. Revenue generated from external clients was VND234bn (+29% YoY), representing 11% of total segment revenue.

## Pillar 4: Super App & online channels

- Online & Super App revenue stood at VND9,564bn in 7M 2026, representing 13% of total DMX revenue. Total Super App registered memberships reached 18.9 million users, adding 1 mn members YTD.

## Pillar 5: EraBlue (Indonesia)

- EraBlue revenue reached IDR2,228bn in 7M 2026, up 89% YoY, with an SSSG of 15%. Network expansion reached a total store count of 283 (+102 stores YTD, +157 stores YoY).
