## Preserved first-page main narrative

- We lower our target price (TP) for POW by 1.1% to VND17,800/share but reiterate our BUY rating. Our slightly lower TP is driven by our (1) a 1 ppt increase in our risk-free rate from 6.0% to 7.0% and (2) 15% QoQ higher net debt balance, which outweigh (3) an 11.2% increase in our aggregate 2026-2030F NPAT-MI forecast and 8% increase in core aggregate NPAT.

- Respective changes of +67%/-1%/+5%/+4%/+4% for our 2026/27/28/29/30F NPAT-MI projections are due to our projection for (1) 25% higher aggregate NPAT from Vung Ang (from EVN’s VND1.6tn payment for its FX losses booked in 2026-27F and 1% higher aggregate sales volume), (2) 16% higher aggregate NPAT from Ca Mau with VND1.4tn of one-off income, (3) 10% higher aggregate NPAT from NT 3&4, (4) 7% higher aggregate NPAT from NT2 (see our NT2 Update Report, dated August 10, 2026), and (5) 12% higher earnings from Hua Na hydropower following its higher financial income and lower SG&A expenses.

- We project 2026F core NPAT-MI to grow 36% YoY to VND3.6tn, driven by (1) 18% YoY core earnings growth from Vung Ang and NT2, (2) NT 3&4 reaching break-even, and (3) strong net Holdco income to outweigh (4) lower YoY earnings from hydropower plants.

- We forecast 2027F core NPAT-MI to grow 14% YoY to VND4.1tn, mainly driven by (1) a strong surge from NT 3&4 on higher sales volume given El Nino weather and 2) 18% YoY higher sector CGM prices, outweighing (3) lower YoY sales volume from Nhon Trach 1, Vung Ang (due to major maintenance), and (4) lower YoY hydropower earnings following El Nino conditions.

- POW’s valuation looks attractive with a 2026F P/E of 8.8x and core P/E of 14.0x, implying a core PEG of 0.7 based on a 2026-30F core EPS CAGR of 20%. The robust core EPS CAGR mainly comes from NT 3&4 with estimated core NPAT of VND1.7tn in 2030 vs break-even in 2026.

- Upside: New projects, p.12; Downside risk: Higher VND depreciation than expected.

                     2026-2030F forecast summary

                     We raise our aggregate 2026-30F reported NPAT-MI forecast by 11% (respective changes
                     of +67%/-1%/+5%/+4%/+4% for 2026/27/28/29/30F) and aggregate core NPAT by 8%, due
                     to our projection for (1) 25% higher aggregate NPAT from Vung Ang (from EVN’s VND1.6tn
                     payment for its FX losses booked in 2026-27F and 1% higher aggregate sales volume), (2) 16%
                     higher aggregate NPAT from Ca Mau with VND1.4tn of one-off income, (3) 10% higher aggregate
                     NPAT from NT 3&4, (4) 7% higher aggregate NPAT from NT2 (see our latest NT2 Update Report),
                     and (5) 12% higher earnings from Hua Na hydropower following its higher financial income and
                     lower SG&A expenses.

                     In this Update Report, we raise our LNG price assumption for 2026-2030F by 4% on average,
                     raise our southeastern gas price assumption for 2026-2030F by 6% on average, and raise our
                     southwestern gas price assumption by 5% on average due to higher Brent and FO price
                     forecasts. Meanwhile, we maintain our coal price forecasts. See our Energy Sector Update,
                     dated July 24, 2026. In addition, we raise most of POW’s ASPs and CGM prices to offset higher
                     input costs, as we believe strong demand in H2 2026 and 2027 will enable POW to pass through
                     100% of the cost increases to EVN.
