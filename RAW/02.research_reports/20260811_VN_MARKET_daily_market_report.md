## News

Key takeaways from Vietcap x KDH Insight Tour Vietcap hosted its Insight Tour with the participation of KDH on August 11, 2026. Discussions

focused on KDH’s project development pipeline, including the Ma Lang and Cho Ga–Gao urban renewal Build-Transfer (BT) project. Overall, management indicated that the company is adjusting its project planning and sales strategies in response to prevailing market conditions to support sales absorption across its portfolio. The strong take-up of the recently launched Gladia high-rise phase, together with KDH’s on-track launch pipeline, should support the company’s presales momentum and earnings outlook. We foresee insignificant changes to our 2026F NPAT-MI (VND1.6tn/USD61mn; +53% YoY) and presales (VND6.7tn/USD254mn; +12% YoY) forecasts, pending a fuller review. Gladia high-rise’s strong absorption supports KDH’s strategy to capitalize on resilient condo demand amid cautious market sentiment. Following the positive launch result of the Gladia high-rise phase (with ~500 condo units officially launched on August 1 and ~90% of launched units presold), KDH is accelerating preparations for the first high-rise launch at Binh

Trung Dong, targeted to launch in Q1 2027G. The company is also reviewing the planning of its existing project pipeline, including potential adjustments to the product mix to place greater emphasis on condominium and affordable commercial housing products to address strong demand from middle-income homebuyers. Updates on projects:

- Gladia by the Waters (11.8 ha; JV with Keppel): - High-rise (616 condos & 23 shophouses): launched official presales from August 1 for ~500 condo units, with ~90% units presold. Expected to launch the second batch in Q4 2026G. Handovers to commence from Q4 2027G. - Low-rise (226 villas & terraces): Around 60% have been presold as of end-Q2 2026. The remaining unsold units are planned to launch in late 2026.

- Binh Trung Dong (18.2 ha; 202 villas/townhouses and ~3,100 condos; adjacent to Gladia): Construction is targeted to start in Q4 2026G, with the first condo phase launching in Q1 2027G and handovers from Q4 2028G.

- Cat Lai (8.2 ha; low rise): Construction is targeted for 2027G and the launch for 2028G.

- The Solina (16.4 ha): Infrastructure works underway; launch targeted for 2027G.

- Ma Lang and Cho Ga–Gao urban renewal BT project: Management believes the project is within KDH’s development and financial capacity, while government support and cooperation with banking partners are expected to facilitate project implementation and funding. For further details, please also refer to our June 29 Vietnam Today report. We currently have a BUY rating on KDH. At the current share price, the stock is trading at 2026/27F P/Bs of 1.0x/0.9x (based on our forecasts), well below its 5-year average of 2.1x. PVS: BOD approves 20% stock dividend for 2025

- PVS’s Board of Directors (BOD) approved a plan to distribute a 2025 stock dividend at a ratio of 100:20 (20 new shares for every 100 existing shares). While the ex-rights date and payment date have not yet been determined, PVS expects the distribution to be completed in Q3–Q4 2026. After the increase, PVS’s share capital will rise 20% to VND6,137bn.

- At the 2025 AGM, PVS outlined a long-term plan to increase its share capital to VND9tn (+47% vs the new share capital of VND6.1tn), and potentially to VND13tn (2.1x the new share capital of VND6.1tn) by 2030.
