AST - H1 NPAT-MI climbs 52% YoY on strong core
operations, associate contributions, above expectations
- Earnings Flash
Company Research 01 Aug 2026
* AST reported Q2 2026 revenue of VND479bn (+22% YoY; -14% QoQ) and NPAT-MI of VND77bn
(+23% YoY; -24% QoQ). This brought H1 2026 revenue to VND1.0tn (+33% YoY; 50% of our
2026F forecast) and NPAT-MI to VND178bn (+52% YoY; 67% of our 2026F forecast). We see
upside potential to our 2026 earnings forecast, pending a fuller review.
* Q2 2026 revenue growth was broad-based across most business lines. In particular, VIPlounge revenue rose +67% YoY and +4% QoQ following the establishment of AST in SGN
Terminal 3 since 2025, remaining a key growth driver, while restaurant/fast-food and duty-free
revenue both increased 15% YoY, respectively. Souvenir revenue was broadly flat YoY, while
hotel and advertising revenue increased modestly.
* In H1 2026, VIP-lounge revenue more than doubled YoY, while restaurant/fast-food and dutyfree revenue increased +34% YoY and +15% YoY, respectively. These higher-margin and
service-oriented businesses accounted for a larger share of revenue growth and supported
consolidated profitability.
* AST operated 141 points of sale at end-Q2 2026, compared with 142 at end-Q2 2025 and 138
at end-Q1 2026. As the store count remained broadly unchanged YoY, strong H1 revenue
growth mainly reflected improved revenue per store, passenger traffic, and a more favorable
business mix.
* Q2 2026 GPM remained strong at 63.8% (flat YoY; -1.8 ppts QoQ), bringing H1 2026 GPM to
64.8% (+1.4 ppts YoY), above our 2026F forecast of 61.9%. We attribute the margin strength to
the favorable international-passenger mix, the growing contribution from the high-margin VIPlounge model, and better operating efficiency across existing outlets.
* SG&A/revenue was broadly stable at 44.3% in Q2 2026 (+0.3 ppt YoY; +2.7 ppts QoQ).
Consequently, Q2 2026 operating profit was VND93bn (+20% YoY; -30% QoQ), with OPM of
19.5% (-0.3 ppt YoY; -4.6 ppts QoQ). In H1 2026, SG&A/revenue remained unchanged YoY at
42.8%, modestly above our 2026F forecast of 42.7% by only 0.1 ppt. Operating profit increased
to VND227bn (+41% YoY; 57% of our 2026F forecast), while OPM expanded to 21.9% (+1.3 ppts
YoY), above our 2026F forecast of 19.2%.

* Profit from associates remained an important earnings contributor at VND15bn in Q2 2026
(+3.2x YoY; +16% QoQ) and VND28bn in H1 2026, equivalent to approximately 4.3x the H1 2025
level and 94% of our 2026F forecast. The stronger contribution was mainly supported by the
continued recovery of VINACS.
* Our view:
- Overall, Q2 results moderated sequentially from a strong Q1 base and under the impact of
Middle East tensions on the aviation industry. H1 2026 results were above our expectations,
mainly driven by stronger-than-expected GPM and associate contributions. We therefore see
upside potential to our 2026 earnings forecast, pending a fuller review.
- AST’s medium-term floor-area expansion remains supported by the Noi Bai T2 expansion and
Long Thanh International Airport projects. However, the timing and earnings contribution of
new outlets will depend on tender results, handover schedules, and airport commissioning.
The limited net store additions in H1 2026 indicate that near-term earnings growth has so far
been driven primarily by the ramp-up and improved efficiency of existing outlets, including
those at SGN Terminal 3.
