BSR releases ambitious 2026–2030 revenue expansion target driven by capacity

upgrade, M&A; earnings guidance remains conservative BSR recently announced its production and business plan for 2026–2030.

Our view: Overall, the plan indicates BSR’s ambition to expand revenue during 2026–2030. However, its earnings targets appear conservative relative to the planned revenue scale. We see insignificant changes to our forecasts, pending a fuller review. Key targets:

- Aggregate 2026–2030 production volume of 38.4 million tonnes (93% of our current forecast), averaging 7.68 million tonnes per year (+8% vs the 2021–2025 average). We see insignificant changes to our volume forecast, as we believe this target is conservative. Annual production of 7.68 million tonnes in 2026–2028 would imply an efficiency rate of around 116%, below the current level of 123%. In addition, BSR plans to increase its capacity by 15.5% following the completion of the Dung Quat refinery upgrade and expansion project before 2029.

- Aggregate consolidated revenue of VND850tn–943tn (USD32.1bn–35.6bn; 105%–116% of our 2026–2030 forecast), averaging VND170tn–189tn p.a. (USD6.4bn–7.1bn p.a.; +25%–39% vs the 2021–2025 average). Aside from the targeted 8% increase in sales volume vs the 2021–2025 average, revenue growth is expected to be driven by new businesses, particularly international trading and M&A. BSR projects aggregate revenue from international trading and M&A of VND103tn–193tn (USD3.9bn–7.3bn) during 2026–2030.

- Aggregate reported NPAT before MI of VND12.9tn–17.8tn (21%–29% of our 2026–2030 forecast), averaging VND2.6tn–3.6tn p.a. (~50% of the 2021–2025 average, ~25% of our forecast). In our view, management’s cautious stance reflects the inherent volatility of the refining business. Historically, BSR’s actual NPAT was often higher than its initial guidance. The actual 2021/2022/2023/2025 NPAT results were 7.7x/10.5x/5.2x/5.2x higher than their initial guidance, respectively. BSR aims to complete the Dung Quat refinery upgrade and expansion project while studying petrochemical, chemical, and green-energy projects in Dung Quat. We currently expect the upgrade and expansion project to be completed and commence operations in 2029, following BSR’s award of the EPC contract in July 2026. Over the longer term, we expect the petrochemical, chemical, and green-energy projects to generate additional revenue, broaden BSR’s product portfolio, and diversify its margins beyond conventional refined products. BSR also outlined potential M&A targets, including Saigon Petro, PVOIL Phu My, petroleum-storage facilities, and PP-storage facilities. Saigon Petro is one of Vietnam’s largest State-owned petroleum enterprises and a major distributor of petroleum products and LPG. As of end-2022, Saigon Petro held around 6% of Vietnam’s petroleum-distribution market (vs around 50% for PLX and 20% for OIL). PVOIL Phu My operates a condensate-processing plant in the Cai Mep Industrial Zone, with annual condensate-processing capacity of 130,000 tonnes and petroleum product output of over 500,000 cbm per year (~13% of OIL’s sales volume). OIL currently owns a 67.64% stake in PVOIL Phu My. These potential transactions could help BSR expand downstream, add distribution and storage infrastructure, and strengthen its presence across the petroleum value chain.

NT2/GAS: Incremental Hai Thach–Moc Tinh gas helps improve NT2’s competitive edge, GAS’s sales volume; slightly positive for stocks

- NT2 released detailed materials seeking shareholders’ written approval for Appendix No. 13 to its gas purchase agreement with GAS. The appendix governs NT2’s purchase of incremental gas supply from the Hai Thach–Moc Tinh expansion project.

- The expansion field is expected to supply a total output of 11.0bn m³ during 2026–2040 for the southeast region, in which NT2 will potentially get a major portion.

- The input gas price is USD7.38/MMBTU (excluding transportation tariff), escalating by 2% annually and without the FO price linkage. We estimate a 2026 delivered gas price of USD8.97/MMBTU (including transportation and distribution cost). We estimate NT2’s new average gas price in 2026-2030 at USD10.9/MMBTU, which is 1% lower than our previous forecast (despite having raised our average Brent oil price and average FO price forecasts by 2% and 7%, respectively, over the same period).

- We note that this means NT2 will use 100% domestic gas at least in the next five years (completely no LNG) and the above new gas price is 23% and 9% lower vs our projections for the LNG price and gas price in the southeast region. This helps to improve NT2’s competitiveness in the CGM market.

- We currently see upside potential for our NT2 2026 earnings forecasts due to (1) lower fuel costs from higher domestic gas supply, (2) strong ASP in H1 2026, and (3) stronger-than-expected Qc volume in H2 2026 (per industry players). We currently have a BUY rating for NT2 with a target price of VND28,800/share.

- Impact on GAS: Slightly positive. Aggregate volume from the Hai Thach-Moc Tinh gas field (current and expansion) is expected to reach 11.4bn m3 over the 2026-2035 period, 74% higher than our current forecast, which strongly supports GAS’s sales volume until 2032. We have a BUY rating for GAS. ## 7. Basel III adoption

- CAR: VIB’s CAR is currently 12.2% under Circular 14 (Basel III) on the standardized approach vs 11.3% under Circular 41 (Basel II). - VIB already complies with the draft amendment to Circular 22/2019, meeting the required roadmap for CDR (<95%), NSFR (>100%), LCR, and the leverage ratio.
