 Record earnings at decade-low multiple
 • We maintain our BUY rating and raise our target price by 39% to VND140,000/share as we roll
   our TP horizon to mid-2027 and raise our respective 2026F/27F/28F NPAT-MI forecasts
   39%/47%/45%, which are partly offset by a 100-bps hike in our house risk-free rate to 7.0%.
 • Our higher NPAT-MI forecasts are driven by higher electronics SSSG and service revenue as
   well as an accelerated pace of BHX store openings.
 • 2026F: NPAT +68% YoY as DMX’s sales jump and BHX reaches 1,200 new stores and 3.4%
   NPM. We forecast DMX/BHX’s respective NPAT at VND10.3tn (+78% YoY)/2.1tn (+158% YoY).
 • 2027F: EPS growth to normalize to 25% as BHX’s expansion is partly offset by dilution from
   the DMX IPO (MWG’s ownership in DMX reduced to 86% from 99% in July 2026). We forecast
   DMX/BHX’s NPAT at VND12.5tn (+21% YoY)/3.1tn (+48% YoY), respectively.
 • MWG trades at 2026F/27F P/Es of 9.2x/7.4x, respectively, which we find attractive against its

   10-year average forward P/E of 13.3x and 2026F-29F EPS CAGR of 18%. Our TP implies 15.6x
   the average 2026-2027 EPS.
 • Investment risks: High memory prices hurting consumer electronics demand; higher interest
   rates that could slow down consumer finance penetration; operational efficiency at BHX.

 BHX: Positive single-digit SSSG, 3.4% NPM in Q2, and 2026 store openings to exceed 1,200
 in 2026. H1 2026 NPAT was VND910bn and revenue grew 25% YoY. BHX opened 632 new stores
 (50% in the north and central combined) in H1 2026 to hit ~3,100 stores. The 2026 cohort (632
 stores) already achieved positive store-level EBIT and began covering DC costs in June. The
 2025 cohort (~800 stores) continues generating positive profit after DC costs. The pre-2025
 cohort (~1,700 stores) delivered single-digit revenue growth with Q2 2026 store-level EBIT up
 over 30% YoY. Northern store openings are progressing smoothly with average revenue per
 store of ~VND1bn/month, meeting expectations. Due to improvements in product assortment,
 logistics, and demand forecasts in 2025, the shrinkage rate and logistics cost ratio improved
 despite higher fuel costs, driving the strong margin expansion in H1 2026.

 DMX: H2 2026 revenue is expected to be maintained at an absolute level similar to H1. Q2
 2026 revenue edged up 1% QoQ despite Q1 being typically a high season. We attribute this to
 upgrade-driven demand and consumer finance penetration, with 38% of DMX’s H1 2026 revenue
 being transacted through installment plans (vs 35% in 2025). NPM surpassed 8% for first time in
 Q2 2026 (+3 ppts YoY to 8.1%) – purely due to GPM. Per DMX, this was driven by market-wide price
 increases (small impact), higher-margin services, cost optimization, and trade-up effects from
 installment programs. We expect the growth rate to normalize in H2 from a higher base as Apple
 upgraded Vietnam to a tier-1 market in Q3 2025, with H2 2026 revenue to grow 15% YoY vs 30% in
 H1. In the medium term, we expect upgrade demand, especially AI-driven demand, to maintain
 ICT revenue growth of ~8% p.a.

                     Valuation

                     Our valuation consists of:
                     • DMX: A 50%/50% mix of 15x target P/E on average 2026F-27F EPS and seven-year DCF model
                       for the electronics business.
                     • BHX: A five-year DCF with 20x terminal P/E.
                     • 0.5x P/S for the pharmacy business.

                     In this Update Report, we roll our TP horizon from end-2026 to mid-2027. We also increase our
                     house risk free rate by 100 bps to 7.0%, bringing our cost of equity to 15.0%

                     DMX successfully raised ~USD500mn mainly from institutional investors in Q2 2026.
                     For DMX, we extend our DCF explicit forecast period from five years to seven years to factor in a
                     2% dilution per year in 2027-2031 as we expect the company to reach its targets in 2025-2030.

                     DMX plans to conduct a long-term stock option program at VND80,000/share. Preliminary five-
                     year targets include doubling net profits and market cap, with maximum dilution capped 10%
                     over five years. The full scheme is under consideration, to be proposed at an EGM in Q4 2026, in
                     which MWG will abstain from voting.
                     For BHX, we reduce our terminal P/E ratio from 25x to 20x in accordance with a higher cost of
                     equity and net profit margins reaching a mature target earlier than previous forecasts.

                     MWG trades at 2026F/27F P/Es of 9.2x/7.4x, respectively, which we find attractive against its 10-
                     year average forward P/E of 13.3x and 2026F-29F EPS CAGR of 18%. Our TP implies 15.6x average
                     2026-2027 EPS. Our standalone valuations for DMX and BHX are USD6.3bn (14.2x P/E) and
                     USD2.5bn (0.95x P/S).
